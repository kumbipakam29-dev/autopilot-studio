Use this template in a **private local file or private workspace**, not in the public repository with real prospect names, emails, phone numbers, or personalized drafts.

## Outreach rules

1. Recheck the business's own public contact channel and the facts used in the message before sending.
2. Send one relevant introduction at a time. Do not bulk-message or scrape private contact data.
3. Ask permission to share a sample rather than attaching a large file to a cold message.
4. If there is no response, wait 5–7 business days and send at most one polite follow-up.
5. Stop immediately after a no or opt-out.
6. Never imply the business is a client, that sample work was commissioned, or that results are guaranteed.
7. Use only owner-provided or properly licensed images and brand assets.
8. Confirm the brief, scope, timeline, and payment terms before starting paid custom work.
9. Record payment only after actual receipt and owner confirmation. Interest or an accepted quote is not a payment.
10. Keep real prospect names, contacts, personalised drafts, and outcomes out of public GitHub repositories.
