## Sending checklist

Before sending any individual draft:
- Confirm the official business social/contact channel from the business's own website/profile.
- Inspect recent posts so the message does not suggest a gap that is not there.
- Replace generic opening with one specific, verifiable detail.
- Keep the first message short; do not attach large files or send repeated follow-ups.
- Ask permission to share a sample rather than claiming results or promising leads/sales.
- User approval is required. Keep each message marked **UNSENT** until a send action is explicitly approved and actually completed.
