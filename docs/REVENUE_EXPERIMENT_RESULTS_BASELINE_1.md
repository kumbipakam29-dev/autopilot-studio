## Visible pay evidence

Three discovered Karmyug public task pages each showed **₹2 per task** at scan time:

- English Script Recording Project: https://work.karmyug.in/jobs/df3d4bf3-c11b-449c-abd6-97e382c5bdf9
- Hindi Script Recording Project: https://work.karmyug.in/jobs/d6f10b46-7249-4484-b114-c8008519acbf
- Bengali Script Recording Project: https://work.karmyug.in/jobs/ed08603e-04a2-4e5e-a053-65c45980bc47

The scan excerpt for the English page showed 129 tasks available and an apply-by date of 31 October 2026, but those figures may change. Re-open the exact official page before deciding. ₹2 per approved task is not a reliable hourly earning estimate: actual time, rejected recordings, task supply, eligibility and payout threshold affect the result. Use only a real human recording with the platform's truthful rules; agents must not fabricate voice or submit tasks for the user.

## Other candidates are leads, not earnings

- OneForma's Paragraph-Level Translation Quality Rater page showed the project as open/accepting applications and listed India among eligible countries in the scan excerpt, but no numeric rate was visible in the extracted evidence. Verify the full current eligibility and rate first: https://www.oneforma.com/projects/paragraph-level-translation-quality-rater/
- YPAI's Data Annotator and Text/NLP Annotator pages showed applications open and described remote India work, but no numeric rate was visible on those exact pages in this run. Do not attribute a pay range from a general promotional page unless it is on the exact current role/invitation: https://freelancer.ypai.ai/jobs/openings/data-annotator-in and https://freelancer.ypai.ai/jobs/openings/text-annotator-in
- OneForma roles that did not list India for that exact project are not to be treated as eligible merely because OneForma has other India-eligible projects.

## Commercial test

A separate local-only prospect pack contains 21 Surat businesses with source URLs, one grounded content angle each and personalised unsent pitch drafts. The priority score estimates visual/content fit, not purchase intent. No profile is claimed to have poor social media, and no message has been sent. Before contacting any business, manually verify its current public page, the detail used in the draft and whether the selected channel permits relevant outreach.

The experimental offer in the pack is ₹499 for 3 post concepts/captions, one 14–20 second Reel script, a 7-day calendar and one revision. This is a test quote, not a verified market average. Do not promise growth, reach, sales or revenue.

## What the experiment proved

1. The scan and queue pipeline ran successfully and generated a shareable public-source action pack.
2. The current scan found only low per-task visible pay in the ₹2-per-task band; it did not confirm any task in the targeted ₹50–₹200 range.
3. The client acquisition lane has research and drafts ready but has not tested real buyer response because no outreach was sent.
4. The system is not a 500-worker autonomous agent fleet. The handlers use deterministic templates and curated page scans; logins, identity checks, applications, work submission, outbound messages, publishing and transfers remain disabled.

## Next experiment

- Prefer exact-role pages with stated pay units and India eligibility over generic landing pages.
- Check whether the effective hourly economics are reasonable before spending time on a low-per-task job.
- Test a small number of personalised client approaches only after the owner manually verifies each profile and message; do not bulk-send.
- Record contacted-by-owner, response, accepted order, delivered work and paid-owner-confirmed separately.
- A payment is real only after the owner confirms the credit in their own payment account; the tracker does not independently inspect bank/UPI accounts.
