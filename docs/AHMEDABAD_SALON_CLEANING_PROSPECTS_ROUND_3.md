## Execution and trust rules
1. Verify the official account/site and current contact channel before sending. Search listings are not always controlled by the business.
2. Ask permission to share a sample. Do not send repeated unsolicited messages.
3. Do not use their logo, photos, customer images or reviews in portfolio material without permission.
4. Label all mock concepts “spec concept — not commissioned.”
5. Log the date/channel only after a message is actually sent; do not mark a reply or sale without evidence.
6. Never promise followers, reach, leads or revenue. The deliverable is content preparation, not guaranteed outcomes.
