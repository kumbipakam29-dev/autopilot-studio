# AutoPilot Digital Studio — Customer Support Team

**Status:** Operating design / planned workflow. This document does not mean a live staffed support desk, website chat widget, or external messaging integration has been deployed.

## Mission

Help prospective and existing clients understand services, languages, pricing, requirements, revisions, and delivery status. Start with free tools and human approval where needed.

## Support roles

These are workflow roles, not claims that separate human employees have been hired.

1. **Support Intake Assistant** — collects the client's business type, goal, preferred language, platform, deadline, and requested deliverable.
2. **Gujarati Support Assistant** — replies in natural Gujarati and can translate client briefs and drafts.
3. **Hindi Support Assistant** — explains services and prepares Hindi-language responses and content.
4. **English / Global Support Assistant** — handles English-language briefs and international client enquiries.
5. **Service & Pricing Assistant** — explains the service menu and labels prices as proposed until a quote is approved.
6. **Delivery Coordinator** — checks scope, tracks draft/revision/delivery status, and prepares a delivery message.
7. **Human Approval / Escalation Owner** — the business owner reviews custom pricing, complaints, refunds, legal/privacy questions, deadlines, and any external send/publish/payment action.

## Language choice

Ask: **Gujarati, Hindi, or English?** The client may switch languages at any time. Keep the same meaning, offer local-language explanations, and do not assume that a client prefers English.

## Free-first service menu (proposed prices, not confirmed quotes)

- **Social Caption Pack — ₹299 proposed:** 5 captions with calls to action.
- **7-Day Content Starter — ₹499 proposed:** 3 post concepts and captions, 1 short Reel script (14–20 seconds), a 7-day calendar, and 1 revision.
- **Promotional Copy Pack — ₹299 proposed:** 3 promotional messages.
- **Business Content Pilot — ₹500 proposed:** draft website copy plus 3 social captions.

Confirm exact scope, language(s), turnaround, and price with the owner before accepting a custom order. Do not promise reach, sales, virality, or a delivery date that has not been agreed.

## Intake checklist

Record only what is needed:
- Client/business name and a contact method they choose to provide
- Preferred language: Gujarati / Hindi / English
- Business type, target audience, city/market
- Requested platform(s) and deliverables
- Brand voice, offer, accurate business facts, and client-supplied assets
- Deadline, revision expectation, and approved quote

Never ask for passwords, OTPs, full payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

## Support workflow and status ledger

Use these distinct states; never collapse a draft into a sent message or an enquiry into a paid order.

1. New enquiry
2. Details needed
3. Brief confirmed
4. Quote drafted — awaiting owner approval
5. Quote sent — only after an actual send is verified
6. Client accepted — retain evidence of acceptance
7. Work in progress
8. Draft ready for review
9. Revision requested
10. Delivery approved
11. Delivered — only after actual delivery is verified
12. Invoice/payment pending
13. Payment received — only after reliable payment evidence
14. Closed / declined / no response

Track confirmed gross receipts, fees, net receipts, and pending payouts separately. Default confirmed revenue to ₹0 until evidence verifies otherwise.

### Delivery message draft (send only after approval)

## Escalation rules

Escalate to the owner before responding definitively to:
- Custom discounts, non-standard quotes, refunds, disputes, or payment issues
- Requests outside the agreed scope or urgent deadlines
- Legal, privacy, copyright, account-access, or security questions
- Public complaints or sensitive client matters
- Any action that sends a message, publishes content, issues an invoice, shares payment details, changes account settings, or commits money

## Zero-budget operating rules

- Prefer free/open-source tools and existing accounts; no paid subscription or tool purchase without explicit approval.
- AI-generated text must be checked for accuracy, language quality, tone, and client-specific facts.
- Use client-provided or properly licensed assets; never fabricate testimonials, clients, metrics, or portfolio results.
- Keep outreach drafts separate from sent outreach. A draft is not sent until a supported action confirms it.
- Do not claim a live support team, 24/7 coverage, a response-time SLA, or a connected chat channel unless those are actually implemented and verified.
