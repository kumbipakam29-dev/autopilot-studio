# AutoPilot Digital Studio — Offer Positioning & 5-Day Ahmedabad Sprint

Prepared: 2026-10-26
Status: internal draft; no public campaign launched and no prospects contacted.

## Positioning decision

Do not present AutoPilot as a full-service social-media agency yet. The strongest honest entry point is a small, clearly scoped **content-preparation service** for busy local owners.

Why: current portfolio contains fictional spec work only. There are no verified client case studies, published client posts, testimonials or sales. Keep the promise limited to deliverables under our control.

### Proposed entry offer — ₹499 (test price; confirm before quoting)

**7-Day Content Starter**
- 3 post concepts with captions
- 2 short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)
- 7-day posting calendar
- 1 revision round
- Delivery in a shareable document
- No account login required; client publishes themselves

Not included: photography/video shoots, finished graphic design files unless explicitly agreed, posting/scheduling, inbox replies, paid ads, follower or sales guarantees.

Before accepting payment, confirm the customer's platform, business details, language, deliverables, deadline and payment terms in writing. Do not collect account passwords.

## Differentiation for small local businesses

1. **Small fixed scope** — an owner can test content help without committing to monthly management.
2. **Local-language option** — offer Gujarati, Hindi-English or English captions when requested; don't assume language preference.
3. **Business-specific ideas** — reference one real menu item/service only after verifying the public page.
4. **Transparent examples** — clearly mark fictional portfolio samples as spec work.
5. **No vanity guarantees** — promise the agreed deliverables, not reach, leads or revenue.

A 2026 Ahmedabad market roundup describes established agency retainers as substantially more expensive than a small deliverables-only pack; these are directional market figures from a third-party article, not a verified price survey. Compete on scope and low commitment, not on claiming to outperform agencies.

## 5-day organic execution sprint

### Day 1 — Make the offer understandable
- Finalize the ₹499 scope and deadline.
- Ensure one-page website has a real contact route before sharing it publicly.
- Keep the Topmate job toolkit separate from the content-service CTA.

### Day 2 — Build one relevant sample
- Pick one Ahmedabad business category.
- Create one original, fictional sample with 1 post concept + caption + 15-second Reel script.
- Label it “SPEC CONCEPT — NOT COMMISSIONED.”
- Do not reuse a business's logo, photos or reviews without permission.

### Day 3 — Permission-first outreach
- Review 5 businesses' official public pages manually.
- Send no more than 5 individually personalized messages through a public business contact route.
- Ask whether they would like one sample concept; do not attach a large unsolicited pitch.
- Record actual send dates and links; never auto-mark as contacted.

### Day 4 — Follow-up only where appropriate
- If no reply, at most one polite follow-up after several business days.
- Stop immediately if asked not to contact.
- If someone replies, clarify needs before promising scope or delivery.

### Day 5 — Review evidence, not vanity metrics
Track: verified messages sent, replies, permission to share sample, quote requests, paid orders, payment confirmation, delivery acceptance.
Do not report a lead as interested until a real reply exists. Do not report revenue until payment is verified.

- A real business contact method is configured and tested.
