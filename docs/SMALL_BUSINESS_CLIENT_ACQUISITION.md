# Zero-budget organic client acquisition — small-business social content

**Business:** AutoPilot Digital Studio  
**Target:** Small businesses in India and internationally  
**Budget:** ₹0; use existing accounts, free portfolio documents and manual, relevant outreach  
**Status:** Prepared outreach materials only. No prospect has been contacted and no post has been published by this document.

## Offer to test

**7-Day Social Content Starter — introductory test quote ₹499**
- 3 post concepts with captions and CTA suggestions.
- 1 short 14–20 second Reel script.
- 7-day content calendar.
- One reasonable revision round.
- Delivery target: within 2 business days after the client supplies accurate business details and approves the brief.
- Client supplies brand assets and publishes content. This package does not include posting, ad spend, photography, video editing, follower guarantees or sales guarantees.

The ₹499 price is a test quote, not a researched market average. Confirm scope and payment terms in writing before starting. Do not promise results you cannot measure.

## Best-fit prospects

Start with owner-operated businesses that post occasionally but have inconsistent captions, unclear CTAs or no simple weekly plan:
- Cafés, bakeries and small restaurants.
- Salons, barbers and beauty studios.
- Home cleaning, repair and local maintenance services.
- Tutors, fitness coaches and small studios.
- Independent boutiques and home-based product businesses.

Avoid scraping private data, bulk unsolicited DMs, automated spam, or pretending to have met the owner. Use public business contact channels and respect each platform's rules and opt-outs.

## Prospect qualification checklist

Before outreach, inspect the public business profile manually:
- Is the business active and genuinely operating?
- Is there a clear public business contact channel?
- Is the content service relevant to a visible need?
- Can you mention one specific, respectful observation without criticising the owner?
- Can you create a small original idea without copying their existing content?
- Is the business in a language/market you can support honestly?

Record only business name, public profile URL, public contact method, the specific observation, date checked and follow-up status. Do not store personal phone numbers or private customer data in the public repository.

## Copy-ready first message

Use only after personalising the bracketed details and checking that outreach is allowed on the chosen platform.

> Hi [Business name] team — I came across your public page and liked [specific, genuine detail about the business]. I run AutoPilot Digital Studio and am testing a small, affordable social-content starter for local businesses: 3 post captions/concepts, one short Reel script and a 7-day content calendar.  
>
> I noticed [one neutral, specific opportunity, e.g. “your latest menu post could also invite people to save the menu or ask about today’s options”]. I can share a small sample idea tailored to your business before you decide. The introductory test package is ₹499, with one revision; you stay in control of publishing. Would that be useful? No worries if not.

## Short version for a public comment or a platform with strict DM rules

> Your [specific post/product] caught my eye. I create simple weekly social-content plans for small businesses. I have a fictional sample portfolio and can share one tailored idea if you’re open to it. No pressure.

Do not paste sales messages into unrelated comment threads. Prefer a public email/contact form when the platform discourages cold DMs.

## Follow-up (one time only)

> Hi [Business name] team — just following up once on my note about a small content starter. If useful, I can send one sample concept based on your public page. If it isn’t a fit, no reply is needed and I won’t keep messaging. Thanks!

If there is no reply, stop. Do not send repeated reminders.

## If a prospect asks for proof

Share [the clearly labelled SPEC portfolio](../portfolio/SMALL_BUSINESS_CONTENT_SAMPLES.md). Say that it contains fictional examples and does not represent paid client work. Never invent testimonials, client names, campaign results, follower growth or previous sales.

## If a prospect is interested

Ask only for information needed to make the draft:
1. Business name and service area.
2. Main product/service and target customer.
3. Preferred language and tone.
4. Confirmed prices, offers, opening hours and contact CTA.
5. Brand photos/logos they have permission to use.
6. Any words, claims or competitors to avoid.

Confirm package contents, ₹499 test price (or another price both sides agree), delivery date, one revision, payment timing and who publishes the content. Ask for only the minimum necessary information. Never request passwords, OTPs or account access for this package.

## Seven-day execution plan

| Day | Activity | Evidence to record |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finalise portfolio and offer; check your own business profile | Portfolio file and accurate offer |
| 2 | Find 10 relevant businesses from public pages | Public profile URLs and a specific observation for each |
| 3 | Personalise and send up to 5 permitted messages | Date/channel/status; no bulk automation |
| 4 | Create 2 additional spec concepts from different niches | Label each SPEC; do not imply client work |
| 5 | Send up to 5 more relevant messages if permitted | Contacted count and responses |
| 6 | Reply to genuine enquiries; clarify scope before doing client work | Agreed scope and status |
| 7 | Review replies and improve offer; follow up once only where appropriate | Replies, opt-outs, proposals and any actual payment evidence |

## Daily scorecard

Track:
- Prospects reviewed.
- Personalised messages sent through permitted channels.
- Replies and positive replies.
- Sample requests.
- Clear quotes sent.
- Paid orders and amount actually received.

Do not count a prospect, reply, verbal interest, invoice, or dashboard estimate as revenue. A first payment counts only after the payment is visible in the receiving account and the user confirms it.
