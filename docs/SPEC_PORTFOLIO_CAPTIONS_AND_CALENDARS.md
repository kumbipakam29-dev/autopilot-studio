**Status:** Fictional spec work for demonstration only. Ember & Bean Café and BrightNest Home Cleaning are invented businesses, not clients. No campaign results are claimed.

## 1. Fictional café: Ember & Bean Café

### Three post captions

**Post 1 — Slow-morning ritual**
Some mornings deserve a slower start. ☕ Find your little pause, take a breath, and make room for a good cup.  
**CTA:** Tag the person you’d bring for a coffee break.  
**Visual direction:** Warm natural light, coffee cup, calm table setting.  
**Alt text suggestion:** A warm cup of coffee on a café table in morning light.

**Post 2 — Menu favourite**
Meet your next coffee-shop favourite: a smooth, comforting sip made for your mid-day reset. What’s your usual order—classic coffee or something new?  
**CTA:** Tell us your go-to order in the comments.  
**Visual direction:** Close-up of a featured drink; add real ingredients and price only after the business confirms them.

**Post 3 — Take a little break**
Your to-do list can wait five minutes. Save this idea for the next time you need a small reset in your day.  
**CTA:** Save this post or share it with your coffee buddy.  
**Visual direction:** Short carousel: café corner, drink close-up, inviting table.

### Two short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)

**Reel A — “Need a reset?” (16 sec)**
- 0–3s: Pouring coffee. On-screen: “Need a little reset?”
- 3–7s: Show a pastry or menu item (only if actually available).
- 7–12s: Slow pan across a cosy seating area.
- 12–16s: Finished drink. On-screen: “Save this for your next break.”
- Caption: A small pause can change the pace of your day. Who’s joining you?
- CTA: Tag your coffee companion.

**Reel B — “What’s your order?” (18 sec)**
- 0–3s: Two drinks in frame. On-screen: “Pick your mood.”
- 3–8s: Close-up of drink A.
- 8–13s: Close-up of drink B.
- 13–18s: Both drinks side-by-side. On-screen: “A or B?”
- Caption: Today’s very important question: which one are you choosing?
- CTA: Comment A or B. Use only real menu items.

## 2. Fictional local service: BrightNest Home Cleaning

### Three post captions

**Post 1 — Room-by-room reset**
A calmer home can start with one small corner. Try this simple reset: clear the surface, group similar items, and finish with the area you use most.  
**CTA:** Save this checklist for your next reset.  
**Visual direction:** A truthful before/after from the business, used only with permission; otherwise use a simple checklist graphic.

**Post 2 — Clear service expectations**
Thinking about booking a home clean? Start by listing your priority rooms and any areas that need special attention. Clear expectations help everyone prepare.  
**CTA:** Send your questions before booking.  
**Visual direction:** Checklist of rooms and priorities. Do not promise services, supplies or results the business has not confirmed.

**Post 3 — Before the appointment**
Three helpful things to do before a scheduled clean: put away valuables, identify priority areas, and mention any surfaces that need special care.  
**CTA:** Save this for your next appointment.  
**Visual direction:** Three-step checklist; avoid showing a customer’s home without permission.

### Two short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)

**Reel A — “Short on time?” (15 sec)**
- 0–3s: Show a busy countertop (staged or used with permission). Text: “Short on time?”
- 3–7s: Show a simple tidy-up sequence.
- 7–11s: Show the final staged space.
- 11–15s: Text: “Make a plan that fits your home.”
- Caption: Small routines can make a space feel easier to manage.
- CTA: Save this tip. Do not present staged footage as a real client result.

**Reel B — “Prep in three steps” (18 sec)**
- 0–4s: Text: “Before your next clean…”
- 4–8s: Step 1: Put away valuables.
- 8–12s: Step 2: Note priority areas.
- 12–15s: Step 3: Mention delicate surfaces.
- 15–18s: Text: “Save this checklist.”
- Caption: A little preparation helps you communicate what matters most.
- CTA: Save or share with someone planning a clean.

## Safe client-work rules
- Confirm business facts, prices, opening hours, service areas and offers with the client before publishing.
- Use only original, licensed or client-authorised visuals, music and logos.
- Never invent reviews, testimonials, before/after results, client names or performance metrics.
- These are draft copy concepts. Posting, design production, account access and ad spend are not included unless separately agreed.
