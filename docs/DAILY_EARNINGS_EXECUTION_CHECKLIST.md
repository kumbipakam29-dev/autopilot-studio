## Before submitting an application
- [ ] Verify the exact role page still says applications open.
- [ ] Confirm India eligibility, language, device, schedule and assessment.
- [ ] Confirm payout method works for the applicant in India; minimum, fees, currency conversion and payout schedule.
- [ ] Read contract and privacy terms; don't upload ID until official domain and purpose are verified.
- [ ] Use truthful answers only. Do not invent experience, education, language fluency or availability.
- [ ] Submit only after personal review. Record APPLIED only after a visible confirmation.
