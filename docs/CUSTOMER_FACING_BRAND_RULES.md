# AutoPilot Digital Studio — Customer-Facing Brand Rules

These rules apply to the website, portfolio samples, outreach messages, proposals, service descriptions, and social posts.

## Brand position

Present AutoPilot Digital Studio as a professional content-preparation service for small businesses. Lead with the client's needs, the deliverables, and the clarity of the process.

## Never expose internal operating constraints

Do not put internal cost strategy or production methods in customer-facing copy. In particular, avoid phrases such as:
- “zero budget” / “₹0 budget”
- “we use free tools”
- “we have no budget”
- references to internal agents, automation limitations, or resource constraints unless directly relevant and deliberately approved

Internal cost controls are an operating decision, not a customer benefit statement.

## What to say instead

- “Practical social media content for small businesses.”
- “Clear deliverables, agreed scope, and human-reviewed drafts.”
- “Captions, short Reel scripts, post concepts, and weekly content calendars.”
- “Introductory package pricing; scope and delivery date confirmed before work begins.”

Only make claims that can be supported. Do not promise followers, reach, leads, revenue, or guaranteed results.

## Portfolio honesty

- Label fictional work as “Concept Portfolio” or “Spec Sample — Fictional Business.”
- Never describe fictional businesses as clients.
- Never invent testimonials, performance metrics, case studies, or publication history.
- Use only original, licensed, or owner-approved assets.
- Replace any example product names, ingredients, prices, availability, and service claims with verified information before preparing work for a real business.

## Pricing and scope

- Treat prices as proposed introductory offers until confirmed by the business owner.
- State exactly what is included and what is excluded.
- Confirm deadline, revision count, payment terms, and any optional services before accepting a job.
- Do not imply that photography, finished designs, account posting, community management, or ad spend are included unless agreed.

## Website launch gate

Before promoting the website publicly:
1. Add an approved business email or contact method.
2. Remove placeholder or nonfunctional contact links.
3. Test the contact route end-to-end.
4. Confirm package prices and scope.
5. Verify the deployed page on mobile and desktop.
6. Keep a clear record of what is live versus still a draft.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] No internal budget/tooling language
- [ ] Clear customer benefit and deliverables
- [ ] All prices and claims verified
- [ ] Fictional samples labelled honestly
- [ ] No broken links or placeholder buttons
- [ ] Contact route tested
- [ ] No unsupported result guarantees
