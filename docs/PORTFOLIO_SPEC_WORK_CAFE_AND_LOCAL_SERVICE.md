# Portfolio Samples — Fictional Spec Work Only

Prepared for AutoPilot Digital Studio. These examples are fictional demonstrations of the proposed service, not commissioned work. The business names, offers, and details below are invented. No client relationship, results, publication, or endorsement is implied.

## Sample A — Café: “Copper Cup Café” (fictional)

**Audience:** nearby students and office workers  
**Offer:** coffee, light bites, relaxed meetups  
**Tone:** warm, conversational, visual-first  
**Format:** Reels target 14–20 seconds; scripts below are 17 seconds.

### Three post concepts and captions

1. **Coffee close-up / product spotlight**
   - Visual: pour shot, cup close-up, café table.
   - On-image headline: “Your little coffee break.”
   - Caption: “A pause, a favourite cup, and a moment to reset. ☕ Save this idea for your next coffee break. [Add verified menu item and location before use.]”
   - CTA: “What’s your go-to coffee order?”

2. **Meet-up moment**
   - Visual: two cups, shared snack, hands reaching for coffee; use original/permissioned imagery only.
   - On-image headline: “Good conversations start here.”
   - Caption: “Catching up doesn’t need a big plan—just a little time together. Tag the person you owe a coffee date.”
   - CTA: “Send this to your coffee buddy.”

3. **Menu discovery carousel**
   - Slides: “Pick your mood” → hot coffee → iced coffee → snack pairing → “Check today’s menu.”
   - Caption: “Hot, iced, or something to snack on? Swipe through this menu-discovery concept and choose your mood. Replace these placeholders with confirmed menu items before publishing.”
   - CTA: “Which slide is your pick?”

### Two 17-second Reel scripts

**Reel 1 — “A 17-second coffee pause”**
- 0–3s: beans / coffee setup. Text: “Need a tiny reset?”
- 3–7s: coffee being poured. Text: “Take a breath.”
- 7–12s: finished cup and table detail. Text: “Make it your coffee moment.”
- 12–17s: full cup / end card. Text: “Save for your next café break.”
- Caption: “A small pause can change the pace of your day. ☕ [Insert verified café details.]”
- Production note: original footage or properly licensed assets only.

**Reel 2 — “Choose your café mood”**
- 0–4s: hot cup. Text: “Cozy?”
- 4–8s: iced cup. Text: “Refreshing?”
- 8–12s: snack close-up. Text: “A little bite?”
- 12–17s: all three options. Text: “What are you choosing?”
- Caption: “Hot, iced, or snack-first? Tell us your pick. [Replace all products with verified menu items.]”

## Sample B — Local service: “BrightFix Home Services” (fictional)

**Audience:** households and renters in the service area  
**Offer:** general home-maintenance visits (fictional)  
**Tone:** clear, practical, reassuring  
**Important:** no safety, licensing, pricing, availability, or guarantee claims without evidence.

### Three post concepts and captions

1. **Service checklist**
   - Visual: clean checklist graphic with three editable placeholders.
   - Headline: “Before you book a home-maintenance visit.”
   - Caption: “A little preparation makes it easier to explain what needs attention. Note the issue, share photos if appropriate, and ask what the visit includes. Replace this generic checklist with your actual service process.”
   - CTA: “Save this checklist.”

2. **How to prepare**
   - Visual: simple three-step graphic: describe the issue → clear access → confirm scope.
   - Headline: “Three steps before your appointment.”
   - Caption: “Make the visit smoother: describe the issue clearly, prepare safe access to the area, and confirm the agreed scope before work begins. Adapt these steps to the services you really provide.”
   - CTA: “Share with someone planning a repair.”

3. **FAQ carousel**
   - Slides: “What should I tell you?” → “Which area needs attention?” → “When did it start?” → “What should I confirm before booking?”
   - Caption: “Clear questions help both sides understand the job. Add your real service area, booking process, and policies before publishing.”
   - CTA: “Message with your question” (draft only; enable only if the business accepts messages).

### Two 17-second Reel scripts

**Reel 1 — “Explain the issue clearly”**
- 0–4s: close-up of a generic household issue illustration. Text: “Need help with a home issue?”
- 4–8s: show note-taking. Text: “Describe what you noticed.”
- 8–12s: show a photo checklist. Text: “Share relevant details.”
- 12–17s: show a blank booking checklist. Text: “Confirm scope before booking.”
- Caption: “A clear description helps start the conversation. Follow the service provider’s instructions and avoid unsafe DIY work.”

**Reel 2 — “Before the visit”**
- 0–4s: checklist graphic. Text: “Before the appointment…”
- 4–8s: write down the concern. Text: “Note the issue.”
- 8–12s: clear a safe path. Text: “Prepare safe access.”
- 12–17s: confirm details. Text: “Check scope and timing.”
- Caption: “Use this general checklist as a starting point. Replace it with the business’s confirmed booking process.”
