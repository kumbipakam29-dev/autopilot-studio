# AutoPilot Studio Revenue Experiment Protocol

**Start date:** 26 October 2026  
**Budget:** ₹0  
**Objective:** Learn whether public-source research and draft-only client acquisition can create a legitimate first paid outcome. This experiment measures results; it does not promise income.

## Work lanes and priority

1. **Small-business content service — primary commercial test.** Research business-level public pages, record evidence URLs and dates, create one specific content angle per prospect, prepare a personalised outreach draft, and produce a clearly labelled SPEC sample. Proposed starter: 3 post concepts/captions, one 14–20 second Reel script, a 7-day calendar and one revision, with a ₹499 experimental quote.
2. **AI data / testing roles — parallel fallback.** Scan official role pages; prioritise clearly stated hourly rates and transparent payout terms. Treat a role as a lead until availability, eligibility, exact rate unit, acceptance criteria and payout route are confirmed.
3. **Existing digital toolkit — separate channel.** Existing product page does not prove a sale. Track page visits and orders only from verified provider-side evidence.
4. **POD — research-only until ready.** Draft concepts and listing metadata; no account creation or live listing uploads.

## Safe free-hand permissions

Agents may perform bounded public-page reads, extract rate/eligibility evidence, rank opportunities with visible reasons, create drafts, generate synthetic/spec samples, run local checks, write local ledgers and reports, and retry failed tests within the repository.

Agents must not log into personal earning platforms, complete identity/liveness tests, record a human's voice, apply or submit tasks on behalf of the user, bypass CAPTCHAs, fabricate qualifications/proof, post public content, send DMs/emails, create or modify accounts, publish marketplace listings, transfer money, or claim a sale without evidence. Approval queues remain review records only; approving a queue entry does not execute the action.

## Scoring the experiment

Track every stage separately:

- lead_found: official/public source exists and has a URL.
- qualified: current opportunity and eligibility/payment terms were checked.
- draft_ready: deliverable or personalised pitch is prepared and reviewed.
- contacted_by_owner: a message was actually sent by the owner through a permitted channel.
- response_received: a real response came back.
- accepted: a client order or provider task was genuinely accepted.
- submitted_by_owner: actual deliverable/task was submitted under the user's identity.
- paid_owner_confirmed: the user checked the actual incoming credit and confirms it.

A higher stage must never be inferred from a lower stage. A lead, application, quote, invoice, pending payout, dashboard estimate or published post is not a payment.

## First review target

Use a small, quality-first batch: 10 carefully qualified business prospects and 2 genuinely suitable role applications, not hundreds of automated contacts. The main commercial outcome is one real buyer conversation followed by a paid small scope. The task-work outcome is one task actually accepted and paid. If 10 permitted personalised contacts yield no qualified enquiry, revise the niche, offer or portfolio rather than increasing message volume.

## Free tool use

Use the deterministic scanner, SQLite local ledger, CSV/Markdown outputs and GitHub Actions test/artifact runs already in the repository. Open-source tool catalog is a discovery guide, not a claim that every listed tool is installed, connected or free of setup costs. Any scheduled workflow added on a feature branch still needs to be merged to the repository's default branch before GitHub's scheduled trigger becomes the recurring default-branch run.

## Record and privacy

- Keep client contact strategy and private payment notes out of public repository history where possible.
- Never put passwords, OTPs, identity documents, bank or UPI credentials, private customer data or payment screenshots in the repository or workflow artifacts.
- Workflow-run SQLite is ephemeral and must not be presented as permanent memory.
- Store only business-level public facts needed to personalise relevant outreach; respect platform terms, anti-spam rules and opt-outs.
- Treat the ₹499 offer as a test quote, not a verified market average. Do not guarantee growth, sales or results.
