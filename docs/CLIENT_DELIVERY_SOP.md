# Client delivery SOP — AutoPilot Digital Studio

**Purpose:** Turn a small-business content enquiry into a clearly scoped, safely delivered paid service.
**Budget rule:** ₹0 upfront. Never buy access to work, leads, tools, or tasks without explicit owner approval.
**Status:** Internal operating guide; not a claim that a client or sale exists.

## 1. Before contacting a prospect
- Verify the business and official public profile.
- Record one specific, accurate observation; avoid generic mass messages.
- Draft one useful content idea tailored to the business.
- Get owner approval before sending anything.
- Do not use private contact data, scrape at scale, or imply a prior relationship.

## 2. Pilot offer (test, not a proven market price)
**7-Day Content Starter — proposed ₹499**
- 7 post ideas and a seven-day calendar.
- 7 editable captions with calls to action.
- 2 short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds each).
- One minor edit round, with delivery timing agreed before payment.
- Excludes photography, video editing, ad spend, posting, guaranteed reach, leads, followers, or revenue unless separately agreed.

Before accepting payment, confirm the scope, deadline, format, revision boundary, and payment method with the customer. Do not represent this offer as validated by market research.

## 3. Discovery questions
1. What does the business sell, and who is the intended customer?
2. Which platform matters most right now?
3. What product/service or offer should this week feature?
4. Are there brand words, claims, images, or topics to avoid?
5. What existing photos/video can the owner provide permission to use?
6. What language and tone suit their audience?

## 4. Production checklist
- [ ] Confirm brief and spelling of business/product names.
- [ ] Create original copy; do not copy competitor captions.
- [ ] Label any fictional concept as spec work in portfolio usage.
- [ ] Check claims, prices, dates, availability, and CTA against customer-provided facts.
- [ ] Provide captions, calendar, scripts, and usage notes in an agreed editable format.
- [ ] Perform a spelling and platform-fit review.
- [ ] Keep one revision round within the agreed scope.

## 5. Payment and evidence
Track these as separate states:
- Enquiry received
- Scope agreed
- Payment requested
- Payment actually received (record amount, date, and owner-verified evidence)
- Work delivered
- Customer confirmed receipt

A payment link, invoice, screenshot of a checkout page, or promise is not proof of received funds. Never mark Paid without actual payment evidence. Do not store full card/bank details or sensitive credentials in the repository.

## 6. Follow-up and privacy
- Follow up only where appropriate and after approval; stop if the person declines.
- Use customer assets only with permission and agreed purpose.
- Store only necessary business contact and project information.
- Do not publish customer work, testimonials, logos, or results without permission.
- Never invent client names, testimonials, metrics, revenue, or case studies.

## 7. Daily metrics
Record counts separately: researched prospects, approved drafts, messages actually sent, replies received, qualified enquiries, proposals, jobs accepted, deliveries, and verified payments. Drafts are not outreach; interest is not a sale; a completed draft is not a payment.
