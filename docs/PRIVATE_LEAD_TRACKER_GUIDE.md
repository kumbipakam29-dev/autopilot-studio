# Private Lead Tracker Template

Use `private_lead_tracker_template.csv` as a starting point, but **save your working copy outside this public repository** (for example, in a private local folder or a private spreadsheet). The committed CSV contains headers only; never add real prospects or contact details to the public repo.

## Suggested status values
- `Not researched`
- `Ready for review`
- `Draft prepared`
- `Contacted`
- `Replied`
- `Interested`
- `Not interested`
- `Do not contact`
- `Quoted`
- `Paid — verified`
- `Delivered`

## Data-minimization rules
- Use public business contact channels, not private/personal contact details unless the person has chosen to provide them for business enquiries.
- Record a short, relevant public observation instead of copying entire profile descriptions.
- Keep opt-outs and honor them; do not contact a business again after a clear request to stop.
- Do not store passwords, access tokens, OTPs, payment account details, government IDs, or private conversation transcripts.
- Mark a payment as verified only after checking the actual payment account.
- Delete information that is no longer needed.

## Weekly counts
Count the number of drafts prepared, messages actually sent, replies received, interested prospects, quotes accepted, payments verified, and deliveries completed. Never treat drafts as sent messages, interest as a sale, or a payment screenshot as cleared funds.
