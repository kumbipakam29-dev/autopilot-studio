## Why these were selected
- Each has a discoverable public business page or listing and a content-relevant product/service.
- This does not prove they want a content provider. The first message should ask permission to share a sample, not presume a need.
- Use only official business channels. For businesses where an official direct route is not verified above, find the official profile first rather than guessing a phone/email.
- Do not copy their logos, photos, customer reviews or brand assets into a portfolio sample without permission. Use original mock visuals and label the work “spec concept — not commissioned.”
- Do not send a generic bulk blast, repeatedly follow up, or mark contacted without evidence.
