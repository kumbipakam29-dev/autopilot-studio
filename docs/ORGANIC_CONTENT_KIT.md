# Organic Content Kit — AutoPilot Digital Studio

**Purpose:** reusable, zero-subscription workflow for creating and preparing small-business social content. This is a template, not proof of published posts, clients, or results.

## Operating rules
- Draft first; a human reviews and approves every public post or direct message.
- Never store passwords, access tokens, customer phone numbers, private lead lists, or personal data in this repository.
- Do not scrape private data, mass-DM, spam, impersonate customers, or claim results that have not been measured.
- Adapt each post to the platform's format and current official API rules. A scheduler does not grant free API access or hosting.
- Keep a local/private copy of client-specific plans; use fictional names in public portfolio examples and label them **spec work**.
- Before posting, check spelling, business details, offer expiry, price, permissions for images/music, accessibility text, and destination links.

## Seven-day starter calendar

| Day | Format | Goal | Prompt / structure | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Static intro | Awareness | Who you serve + one clear benefit + location | Follow for useful tips |
| 2 | Reel (14–20 sec) | Reach | Hook (0–3s), 2–3 useful shots (3–14s), CTA (last 3–6s) | Save this idea |
| 3 | Carousel | Education | 3 common mistakes and one practical fix for each | Save/share |
| 4 | Story / short post | Conversation | One simple poll or this-or-that question | Reply with a choice |
| 5 | Offer post | Leads | Specific offer, what's included, terms, valid dates | Message for details |
| 6 | Behind-the-scenes | Trust | Show a real process, product, team, or preparation | Ask a question |
| 7 | FAQ / proof | Conversion | Answer one real FAQ or share verified feedback with permission | Contact the business |

Do not invent testimonials, customer counts, ratings, scarcity, discounts, or before/after results. If no real proof is available, publish a helpful FAQ instead.

## Short Reel script template (14–20 seconds)
- **0–3s — Hook:** “Still struggling with [specific customer problem]?”
- **3–10s — Value:** show two or three real product/service details; use concise on-screen text.
- **10–15s — Benefit:** explain one realistic, verifiable benefit.
- **15–20s — CTA:** “Save this” / “Ask us about [service]” / “Visit [verified link].”
- Add captions/subtitles, check music rights, and use footage the business owns or has permission to use.

## Copy-ready caption template
**Hook:** [one customer question or useful insight]

**Value:** [one concrete tip or clear description of the service]

**Details:** [verified location, price/terms if applicable, and what is included]

**CTA:** [one action only]

**Hashtags:** [3–5 relevant, specific tags; do not stuff unrelated trending tags]

Replace every bracketed field and verify all claims before publishing.

## Ethical first-contact message
Hello [Business Name] team — I’m preparing social-content ideas for local businesses. I noticed [specific, public, non-sensitive observation]. I can share one no-obligation sample idea for [their service/product]. Would that be useful? If not, no worries — I won’t follow up.

Use only an appropriate public business contact channel, personalize each message, respect platform rules, and stop if asked. Do not automate bulk unsolicited messages.

## Follow-up tracker fields
Keep this in a private spreadsheet, not in this public repository:
- Business name
- Public business contact URL
- Niche / city
- Specific observation
- First contact date
- Status: Not contacted / Contacted / Replied / Interested / Not interested / Paid
- Next action date
- Consent / opt-out note
- Revenue received (only after verified payment)

## Weekly measurement
Record actual values only: posts published, reach/impressions, saves, shares, profile visits, enquiries, qualified leads, paid orders, and revenue. Mark unavailable metrics as **not available** rather than zero. Do not promise reach or sales.

## Free-first production workflow
1. Research the business from its public website/profile; record only relevant business facts.
2. Draft one week of platform-specific copy and a simple shot list.
3. Run the checklist above and review links and claims.
4. Export drafts to Markdown/CSV or a client-approved document.
5. Client approves final copy and media.
6. Publish manually or use a verified, supported scheduler integration only after the account owner authorizes it.
7. Record the actual result and learnings.
