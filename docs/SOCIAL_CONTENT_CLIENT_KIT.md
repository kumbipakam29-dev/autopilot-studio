# Social Media Content Starter — Client Acquisition Kit
Updated: 2026-10-26
Owner: AutoPilot Digital Studio
Budget: ₹0 upfront

## Offer (test price, not a proven market rate)
**7-Day Social Content Starter — suggested pilot price ₹499**
- 7 post ideas arranged in a 7-day calendar
- 7 captions with simple calls to action
- 2 short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)
- One revision round
- Delivery as a simple PDF/Google Doc; no paid design tool required

This is a proposed pilot offer, not a proven price or guaranteed sale. Confirm the scope and final price with the client before work starts. Do not promise follower growth, leads, sales or reach.

## Honest portfolio rule
Create two fictional sample businesses (café/restaurant and local service provider). Put this note visibly on each sample:
**SPEC WORK — fictional example created to demonstrate style; not a client project and not evidence of business results.**
Never add invented testimonials, logos implying partnership, metrics or customer names.

## Short outreach message
Hi [business/team], I noticed [specific factual observation about a recent public post or page]. I create practical social content for small businesses—captions, post concepts and short Reel scripts. I can share a small fictional sample so you can judge the style. If useful, I offer a 7-day starter with a content calendar, captions and two short Reel scripts. Would you like to see the sample? No pressure if it’s not a fit.

## Follow-up (send only once, after a reasonable interval)
Hi [business/team], just following up once on my message about a small 7-day content starter. If this isn't a priority, no worries and I won't follow up again. Thanks.

## Prospect selection checklist
Only add a prospect when:
- The business is real and its public business page/contact route is confirmed.
- There is a specific, visible content opportunity (e.g. inconsistent posting, no captions, service not explained clearly). Be respectful; don't insult their work.
- The message includes a real observation, not a generic claim.
- The contact method is explicitly provided for business inquiries or is a public business contact.
- The prospect has not opted out and has not already received repeated messages.

## Lead tracker columns
business_name, niche, city_or_market, public_profile_url, public_contact_route, factual_personalization, opportunity_note, draft_message, owner_approved, sent_by_owner, sent_date, reply_status, next_action, opt_out, last_checked_date.

## Delivery workflow
1. Ask the client for target audience, service/menu details, brand voice, preferred language, offers and restrictions.
2. Draft the calendar and content.
3. Quality check spelling, factual business details, platform requirements and image rights.
4. Send preview; wait for approval.
5. Make one included revision.
6. Deliver final files after the agreed payment terms are clear.
7. Record payment only after the owner verifies receipt.

## Quality checklist
- No unsupported promises or invented claims.
- No copied competitor captions or unlicensed images/music.
- CTA is clear and appropriate.
- Dates, prices, locations and offers are supplied/confirmed by client.
- Spec samples are labeled.
- Client approves all publication-ready material; AutoPilot does not publish to accounts without explicit authorization.

## First 7 days
1. Finish two clearly labelled spec samples.
2. Select 5 suitable businesses using public information; record the factual reason for each.
3. Personalize one draft per prospect.
4. Review each message manually; no bulk messaging.
5. Send only after owner approval.
6. Log replies, opt-outs and next steps.
7. Adjust offer based on actual responses; do not describe no response as a sale or validation.
