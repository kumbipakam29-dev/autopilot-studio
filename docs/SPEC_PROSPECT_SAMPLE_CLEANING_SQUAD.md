## Sample post 1 — Home deep cleaning

**Format:** 4-slide carousel

- Slide 1: “Planning a home deep clean?”
- Slide 2: “Start with the spaces that need extra attention.”
- Slide 3: “Ask about a home deep-cleaning plan suited to your space.”
- Slide 4: “Need details? Contact the team to discuss your cleaning requirements.”

**Caption draft:**  
A home deep clean starts with understanding your space and what needs attention. Whether you’re preparing for a reset or planning regular upkeep, make a list of your priority areas before booking.

The Cleaning Squad lists home deep cleaning among its services. Contact the team to confirm the right service scope for your home.

**CTA:** “Send your questions directly to the team.”  
**Fact-check before publishing:** confirm service inclusions, available areas, booking method, and any minimum property-size requirements. Avoid claiming a specific result or turnaround time unless confirmed.

## Sample post 2 — Sofa & carpet cleaning

**Format:** single image or 3-slide carousel

- Slide 1: “Your sofa gets everyday use. When did it last get a proper clean?”
- Slide 2: “Different fabrics and stains can need different approaches.”
- Slide 3: “Ask about the suitable sofa or carpet cleaning option for your material.”

**Caption draft:**  
Spills, dust and everyday use can build up over time. Before arranging sofa or carpet cleaning, share the material and the kind of issue you want addressed so the team can advise on the suitable service.

Sofa and carpet cleaning are listed on The Cleaning Squad’s website. Confirm fabric suitability and stain-treatment limitations with the service team first.

**CTA:** “Ask the team about your sofa or carpet.”  
**Fact-check before publishing:** do not promise stain removal, sanitisation, fabric safety, or a particular before/after result without verified evidence and approved wording.

## Sample Reel — 16–18 seconds

**Concept:** “Three things to confirm before booking a deep clean”

- 0–3 sec: Text on screen: “Booking a deep clean?”
- 3–7 sec: Shot of a written checklist: “1. Which rooms or areas?”
- 7–11 sec: Shot of checklist: “2. Any priority stains or surfaces?”
- 11–15 sec: Shot of checklist: “3. What is included in the service?”
- 15–18 sec: End card: “Confirm the details with your cleaning provider.”

**Voiceover:** “Before booking a deep clean, list the areas you want covered, flag any priority surfaces, and confirm exactly what the service includes. A clear brief helps you ask better questions before you book.”

**Production note:** use original footage or owner-approved/licensed visuals only. Do not show workers, customer homes, private belongings, or before/after comparisons without explicit permission. This is an educational draft, not a claim about the company's current process.
