# First Verified Payment Sprint (India, zero budget)

**Last official-page check:** 2026-10-24. Recheck each page before acting; openings, pay and eligibility can change.

This is an execution checklist, not an income promise. AutoPilot-Studio discovers public leads and prepares QA/proposal materials; it cannot create accounts, complete personal qualification tests, record your voice, submit platform work, or inspect your bank/UPI app. A lead counts as income only after the provider accepts the work and you confirm the actual credit in your own payment app.

## Priority order

### 1) Karmyug AI — potentially quickest phone-first task route
- Official site: https://work.karmyug.in/
- The live page displayed Hindi Script Recording, English Script Recording and Bengali Script Recording at ₹2 per approved task when checked. It says payout is weekly by UPI/bank and other listed methods, and that joining is free.
- The provider says it checks identity before paid work and reviews task quality. Complete any ID/liveness step only on the official site/app you navigated to yourself. Never pay a deposit or unlock fee.
- First actions: create your own account; select only languages you genuinely speak; complete the official qualification; read the task's script, payment and acceptance rules; do a small batch carefully; check approval status and payout threshold. Do not use generated voice or let an agent submit personal voice work on your behalf.
- Reality check: ₹2 is per approved task, not per minute; the total depends on accepted volume, project availability, and payout rules. The public listing is not proof that your account has access to the task.

### 2) YPAI — India annotation / language projects
- [Data Annotator, India](https://freelancer.ypai.ai/jobs/openings/data-annotator-in)
- [Text and NLP Annotator, India](https://freelancer.ypai.ai/jobs/openings/text-annotator-in)

The exact pay and available hours are stated in an individual project invitation; application review and project selection are not guaranteed.
- First actions: apply only for roles that honestly fit your language/device skills; use your real details; read each invitation's rate unit, acceptance criteria and payout terms before accepting. Never invent qualifications.

### 3) UserTest Pro — higher-value studies when matched
- Official page: https://www.usertestpro.com/get-paid-to-test
- The public page describes India-wide website/app/experience studies, says invites show time and payout, and advertises weekly rewards via Tremendous as gift card or UPI. Its page displayed video studies at ₹300–₹2,500+; this is a study-specific advertised range, not guaranteed work or earnings.
- First actions: register on the official site, complete the profile truthfully, accept only studies you qualify for, and check the exact payout and time before starting. Never fake demographics or use AI to impersonate a participant.

## Verification standard

A source page being open, a listed hourly rate, an application, a dashboard balance, or a submitted task is not a verified payment. A first payment is verified only when: (1) the provider accepts work, (2) the provider records/announces the payout, and (3) you confirm the credit actually arrived. The local ledger deliberately requires your confirmation for the final stage.

## Stop conditions / scam protection

Stop and do not proceed if anyone asks for an upfront fee, deposit, payment to unlock a job, account rental, OTP/password, fake reviews, fake proof, or a CAPTCHA bypass. Do not make unnecessary financial transactions for testing. Keep personal identity and payment data out of this repository.
