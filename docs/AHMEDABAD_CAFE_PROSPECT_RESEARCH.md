## Prospect 1 — Binileaf Café
- Official sources: https://binileaf.com/ and https://binileaf.com/contact-us/
- Verified public details: University Area, Ahmedabad; Instagram handle @binileaf. The official site describes the name as combining coffee beans and tea leaves, tells a founding story about six school friends, and lists specialty drinks including Coconut Matcha, Watermelon Tonic Matcha, and Mango Coconut Lemonade.
- Personalization opportunity: its own “beans + leaves” identity and founding story provide specific angles, without assuming anything about its current social posting quality.
- Sample content idea (draft only):

  - **Caption:** “Some days call for a bold coffee; others, a refreshing tea. Which side are you on today? Tell us your pick below.” Confirm that the featured drinks are currently available before publishing.
- Other angles: a founder-story carousel based on approved facts; a signature-drink Reel with a clear 14–20 sec shot list.
