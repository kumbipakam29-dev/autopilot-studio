# Prospect Qualification Scorecard — Ahmedabad-first

**Purpose:** prevent weak, stale, or speculative leads from being treated as qualified prospects. This is a manual review tool, not a claim that a business needs help. Complete it before preparing personalized outreach.

## Hard gates (any failure = HOLD)

- [ ] Business identity and Ahmedabad location matched to an official or otherwise credible public source.
- [ ] Current public contact route checked directly on an official website or business-controlled profile.
- [ ] No assumption that the business has a content problem; inspect current public content before recording any gap.
- [ ] Personalization fact is specific, factual, non-sensitive, and still visible at time of review.
- [ ] Any proposed portfolio image/video uses original or licensed material; no copying customer photos, logos, menu images, or reviews without permission.
- [ ] Outreach is one-to-one, truthful, and includes no fabricated results, client names, guarantees, or fake urgency.
- [ ] Draft remains unsent until the user explicitly approves that exact message and channel.

## Score each dimension 0–2

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source quality | Unclear/aggregator only | Mixed sources | Official business-controlled source confirmed |
| Contact route | None found | Public route but ownership unclear | Official public enquiry/contact route confirmed |
| Personalization | Generic or inferred | Broad product/service fact | Specific current fact verified on official source |
| Service fit | No clear fit | Plausible content use case | Concrete content asset opportunity grounded in current facts |
| Rights-safe sample | Would require unlicensed assets | Can use generic mock-up | Can create original spec work without implying endorsement |
| Recency | Not checked | Checked but date unclear | Rechecked immediately before drafting |

**Maximum:** 12. This is a prioritization aid, not a prediction of conversion.

- **10–12:** eligible for human review only if every hard gate passes.
- **7–9:** research more; do not send.
- **0–6:** hold or remove from the active shortlist.
- **Any hard-gate failure:** HOLD regardless of score.

## Status and payment integrity

- Research candidates are not qualified leads until the hard gates and score are recorded.
- A draft is not a sent message; a sent message is not a reply; a reply is not a client; a client is not a payment.
- Record a payment as verified only after the user confirms the money is actually credited in their own bank/UPI/payment app. Never infer payment from an invoice, screenshot of a listing, platform dashboard, or verbal promise.
- Do not mass-message, scrape private data, bypass platform limits, or contact businesses without explicit user approval.
- No subscriptions, paid APIs, paid ads, or purchases without explicit approval.

## Recommended operating sequence

1. Research five businesses maximum in one niche/area.
2. Check official sources and current public profiles individually.
3. Score and hold weak candidates rather than inflating the list.
4. Create one useful, original sample tailored to the strongest candidate.
5. Draft one short message with one clear offer; do not send without approval.
6. Log replies and next steps accurately; improve the sample based on actual feedback.
7. Expand from Ahmedabad to Gandhinagar only after this loop is repeatable.
