# AutoPilot Digital Studio — Offline Client Acquisition Kit

**Status:** Draft materials only. No businesses have been contacted. No client, sale, or result is implied.
**Budget:** ₹0. **Service being tested:** 7-Day Content Starter — proposed ₹499; validate demand before treating this price as established.

## 1. Clear offer
For a small business that already has photos/videos but struggles to plan posts:
- 7-day social content calendar
- 7 post concepts with captions and calls to action
- 2 short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)
- One minor revision round
- Delivery date and payment terms agreed in writing before work begins

Not included unless separately agreed: graphic design files, filming, account login, publishing, ad management, community management, guaranteed reach/followers/leads/sales.

## 2. Best first niches
Start with a narrow niche for each batch:
1. Cafés, bakeries and dessert shops
2. Home services (cleaning, repair, tutors)
3. Salons and independent fitness/wellness providers

Prefer owner-operated businesses with an active public Instagram page and visible recent posts. Look for a specific content opportunity (inconsistent posting, no clear FAQ posts, repeated product shots without useful captions). Do not claim their performance is poor unless analytics are public and actually checked.

## 4. Personalised first message (copy and edit)
Hello [Business name] team — I’m building AutoPilot Digital Studio and preparing practical social-media content for small businesses. I noticed [specific, accurate public detail]. One idea for your page could be [one useful, concise content idea].

I’m testing a small 7-day starter: a week-long content calendar, 7 caption/post ideas and 2 short Reel scripts, with one minor revision. Proposed introductory price: ₹499. No promise of follower growth or sales. Would a short example of the format be useful?

If not relevant, no worries — I won’t keep following up.

**Before sending:** verify the detail, use only the business's published contact channel, personalise the message, and do not send until the owner has approved outreach.

## 6. Common replies
**“Send details.”**
Thanks! The starter is 7 post ideas/captions, a 7-day calendar and 2 short Reel scripts (14–20 sec), with one minor revision. The proposed test price is ₹499. If that suits you, I’ll confirm the brief, deadline and payment terms before starting.

**“Can you guarantee growth?”**
No — I can guarantee only the agreed deliverables and deadline, not platform reach, followers, leads or sales.

**“Can you post it for us?”**
The starter is content planning and copy only. Publishing or account access would need a separate discussion and explicit scope.

**“Too expensive / not now.”**
Understood, thanks for letting me know. I won’t pressure you. If useful later, you can keep my details and reach out.

**No reply:** At most one polite follow-up after 4–7 days, if the channel and context make that appropriate. Stop after that; honour any opt-out.

## 7. Delivery checklist after a client accepts
1. Confirm business name, target audience, platform, tone, services/products and any verified prices/offers.
2. Ask for permission to use any supplied images/brand assets; avoid account passwords.
3. Confirm exact deliverables, deadline, revision limit, price and payment timing in writing.
4. Draft captions/calendar/scripts; flag every fact that needs client verification.
5. Deliver in a simple document; ask for one consolidated revision.
6. Record payment only after checking actual payment evidence. Do not label a lead as a customer or a promised payment as paid.

## 8. Weekly zero-budget experiment
- Day 1: choose one niche and review 10 public business profiles manually.
- Day 2: select 3–5 relevant prospects and write one specific idea per business.
- Day 3: owner reviews and approves each message.
- Day 4: send only the approved personalised messages.
- Day 5–7: track replies, objections and follow-up dates; improve the offer based on actual feedback.

Track: prospects reviewed, messages approved, messages actually sent, replies, requests for details, accepted briefs, completed deliverables, and evidenced payments. Never infer outcomes from drafts or views.

## 9. Free tool options
- Drafts and portfolio: local Markdown/LibreOffice or Google Docs free tier.
- Design: Canva free tier or LibreOffice Impress; check asset licensing.
- Static preview: local HTML file or repository draft; do not publish until contact, hosting eligibility, QA and owner approval are resolved.
- Tracking: local CSV/LibreOffice spreadsheet; keep personal data minimal.
- Website alternatives: a local HTML preview is fully free but not public; GitHub Pages is static and eligibility/terms/settings must be checked before business use; free tiers of other hosts can change and may impose limits. Do not promise a free tier will remain free or treat a form without a backend as working lead capture.

## 10. Evidence rules
A prospect list is research, not outreach. A draft is not a sent message. A sent message is not a reply. A checkout link is not a sale. A payment is “verified” only after evidence is checked by the owner.
