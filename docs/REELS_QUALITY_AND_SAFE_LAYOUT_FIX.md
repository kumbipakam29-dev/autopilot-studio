# Reels Quality Fix — Safe Text, Real Footage, No Filler

**Purpose:** prevent text spilling outside frames and replace generic/low-quality promo clips with short, specific, shootable ideas.
**Status:** production checklist and scripts only. These are not rendered videos, and nothing has been published.

## Non-negotiable export rules
- Canvas: vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, MP4/H.264, 30 fps.
- Keep every text line, logo and key subject away from UI overlays. Conservative ad-safe guide: keep the top 14%, bottom 35%, and 6% on both sides free of text/logos (Meta guidance for ads; organic UI can vary).
- Use a central text column no wider than about 75% of canvas; align left or center consistently.
- Maximum 4–6 words per overlay; no paragraphs, no tiny fonts, no text hugging edges.
- One idea per shot. Keep each overlay on screen long enough to read; use 2–4 shots total for a 14–20 sec Reel.
- Captions must be checked on a phone preview before delivery. Reject if any glyph, logo, face, product, or CTA is clipped/covered.
- Do not use random stock footage, fake reviews, fake customer reactions, fake offers, invented prices, or unlicensed music. If real footage is unavailable, label the output as a storyboard/spec concept—not a finished video.

## Fast review gate (fail = revise)
1. Open the exported MP4 on a phone, full-screen.
2. Check first frame, every text change, and final frame.
3. Confirm text sits inside safe area; lines do not wrap awkwardly; contrast is strong.
4. Confirm the footage visibly matches the line on screen.
5. Remove filler transitions, unrelated clips, overused zooms and generic AI imagery.
6. Play once muted: story should still make sense through readable text.
7. Play with sound: voice/music must not drown each other.
8. Final CTA is one clear action, not a fake button.

## Spec Reel A — Bakery / custom cake order journey (15 sec)
**Footage needed:** actual baker hands, cake detail, box/packing. Use only footage the business owns or has permission to use.

- 0–2 sec: close-up of icing. Overlay: **“A cake made for your moment”**
- 2–6 sec: piping/decorating detail. Overlay: **“Made to order”**
- 6–10 sec: finished cake rotation, steady shot. Overlay: **“Details matter”**
- 10–13 sec: packaging. Overlay: **“Packed with care”**
- 13–15 sec: clean final cake shot. Overlay: **“Ask about custom orders”**

**Voiceover:** “From the finishing touches to careful packing, every detail matters. Ask us about a custom cake for your next celebration.”
**Caption:** “Planning a celebration? Share your date, serving size and design inspiration when you enquire. Confirm availability and details directly with the bakery.”
**Do not claim:** delivery, freshness guarantees, specific ingredients, prices, or turnaround unless the business confirms them.

## Spec Reel B — Cafe visit / atmosphere (16 sec)
**Footage needed:** real exterior/signage, ambience, actual food/drink, table/interior. No invented menu item or prices.

- 0–2 sec: exterior/signage. Overlay: **“Need a change of scene?”**
- 2–6 sec: one calm interior shot. Overlay: **“A little time to unwind”**
- 6–10 sec: close-up of an actual item confirmed by the cafe. Overlay: **“Your pick, your pace”**
- 10–13 sec: detail shot of seating/lighting. Overlay: **“Meet. Pause. Recharge.”**
- 13–16 sec: wide shot of venue. Overlay: **“Save this for your next visit”**

**Voiceover:** “For a catch-up, a quiet break, or a change of scene—keep this spot in mind for your next cafe visit.”
**Caption:** “Tag the person you’d bring along. Check the cafe’s current menu and opening hours before visiting.”
**Do not claim:** “best”, “viral”, customer popularity, ambience details, menu names, or opening hours without verification.

## Typography/layout defaults
- Use a clean sans-serif (e.g. Inter/Arial), bold headline, high contrast.
- Target headline 48–64 px on 1080×1920; supporting text 36–44 px. Adjust only after phone preview.
- Maximum two lines; use manual line breaks, e.g. “A cake made / for your moment”.
- Keep text block around x=120–820 and y=300–1180 as a conservative working box; never position important copy in the bottom UI region.
- Use a subtle solid/gradient backing only when footage reduces contrast. Avoid decorative text animations that make reading harder.

## Deliverable checklist
- [ ] Actual video file attached/exported and playable
- [ ] Rights/permission confirmed for every clip and audio track
- [ ] Safe-zone preview checked on phone
- [ ] Spelling, line breaks and contrast checked
- [ ] Claims/menu/prices/hours approved by business
- [ ] CTA and caption proofread
- [ ] Client approval recorded before posting
