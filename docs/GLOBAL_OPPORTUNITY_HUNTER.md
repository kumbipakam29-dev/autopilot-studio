## Scam and privacy checks
- Never pay registration, training, security-deposit, activation, or task-unlock fees.
- Do not fake location, identity, credentials, or language fluency.
- Check minimum withdrawal, payout rails available in India, currency conversion, tax obligations, and whether the platform is the actual contracting party.
- Never share passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, or remote banking access.
- Do not upload identity documents until the official domain, privacy policy, and contract are checked.
- For transcription tasks, follow the platform's rules on AI tools; some explicitly prohibit machine transcription.
- Avoid tasks that request fake reviews, spam, account rental, money forwarding, or unauthorized access.
