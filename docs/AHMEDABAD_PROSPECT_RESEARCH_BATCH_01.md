## Before any message is sent
1. Manually open the source/profile again and confirm the business is active.
2. Review the latest public posts yourself; note one real, specific detail and one constructive content idea.
3. Confirm the contact channel is publicly offered for business enquiries.
4. Ask the business owner for explicit approval of each exact message before sending.
5. Record actual send date and evidence only after sending. Do not bulk-message or repeatedly follow up.
6. Respect no-reply/opt-out; at most one polite follow-up after 4–7 days if appropriate.
