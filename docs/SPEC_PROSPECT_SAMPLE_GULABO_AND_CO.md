## Sample post 1 — “A coffee mood for every kind of day”

**Format:** 4-slide carousel

- Slide 1: “What’s your coffee mood today?”
- Slide 2: “Classic and bold? Madras Filter Kaapi.”
- Slide 3: “A familiar favourite with a twist? Jaggery Cappuccino.”
- Slide 4: “Something different? Ask the team what’s on the menu today.”

**Caption draft:**  
Some days call for a familiar cup; other days are for trying something a little different. The current menu lists Madras Filter Kaapi and Jaggery Cappuccino. Which one would you pick for your next café break?

**CTA:** “Tell us your pick: Filter Kaapi or Jaggery Cappuccino?”  
**Fact-check before publishing:** confirm both items are currently available and approve the final names/spelling. Do not call either a bestseller or make taste/health claims without evidence.

## Sample post 2 — “An iced drink worth exploring”

**Format:** single image or 3-slide carousel

- Slide 1: “Your next iced-drink pick?”
- Slide 2: “Lychee Espresso”
- Slide 3: “Guava Peach Iced Tea”

**Caption draft:**  
In the mood to explore beyond your usual order? The menu currently lists Lychee Espresso and Guava Peach Iced Tea. Save this idea for your next visit, and ask the team which options are available today.

**CTA:** “Which would you try first?”  
**Fact-check before publishing:** confirm availability, correct drink photography, ingredients, and menu naming. Do not imply either drink is new, seasonal, sugar-free, or available all day unless confirmed.

## Sample post 3 — “A savoury bite or a sweet finish?”

**Format:** 4-slide carousel

- Slide 1: “Savoury first or dessert first?”
- Slide 2: “Avocado Thecha Sourdough Toast”
- Slide 3: “Burnt Jaggery Cheesecake”
- Slide 4: “Which one is your café order?”

**Caption draft:**  
A savoury café bite or something sweet to finish? The current menu lists Avocado Thecha Sourdough Toast and Burnt Jaggery Cheesecake. Pick your side in the comments—or ask the team what is available today.

**CTA:** “Savoury or sweet?”  
**Fact-check before publishing:** confirm item availability, ingredients, dietary/allergen information and approved images. Do not describe ingredients beyond the menu or make nutritional claims.

## Sample Reel — 15–18 seconds

**Concept:** “Build your café order in three quick choices”

- 0–3 sec: Text: “Choose your café mood”
- 3–7 sec: Menu card/approved shot: “Coffee: Madras Filter Kaapi or Jaggery Cappuccino”
- 7–11 sec: Menu card/approved shot: “Iced: Lychee Espresso or Guava Peach Iced Tea”
- 11–15 sec: Menu card/approved shot: “Finish: Burnt Jaggery Cheesecake”
- 15–18 sec: End card: “Which combination would you pick?”

**Voiceover:** “Start with your coffee mood, switch it up with an iced drink, then decide: savoury bite or something sweet? Which combination would you choose?”

**Production note:** use only owner-approved or properly licensed photos/video. Keep names legible and avoid showing a dish/drink that is not the exact menu item. This is a concept, not a claim that any item is available at the time of posting.
