## Qualification rules before any outreach

- Official-source candidates are not automatically qualified prospects. Check current operation, official social profiles, recent posts, contact preferences, and service fit.
- Do not infer that a business needs help because it has few posts or low engagement. Record observable facts only.
- Create a sample post or Reel only as a clearly labelled speculative concept. Do not use logos or photos without permission; prefer original generic mockups until rights are clear.
- Keep outreach one-to-one, truthful, relevant, and respectful. No bulk messaging. User approval is required before sending.
- No contact, sale, payment, or published work is claimed in this document.
