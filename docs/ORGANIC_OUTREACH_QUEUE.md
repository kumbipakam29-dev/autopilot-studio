## Routine operating rules

- Keep outreach one-to-one, relevant, and respectful. Never mass-spam.
- Recheck the official contact page/profile before sending because contact details and menu items can change.
- Use only business-facing contact channels that the business itself publishes.
- One initial introduction; at most one polite follow-up if there is no reply after 5–7 business days. Stop immediately after a no, opt-out, or no further contact request.
- Never imply a prospect is a client or that a sample was commissioned. Keep prospect samples private until permission is given.
- Do not attach large files in the first contact. Ask whether they would like to see the sample; share a link only if appropriate.
- Do not claim recent social media weakness without reviewing current posts and noting a specific, accurate observation.
- Track only observable facts: contact date, reply, opt-out, quote, work status, and owner-confirmed payment.

## Response handling

- **Interested:** share the relevant sample; ask which platform, audience, preferred language, brand assets, factual details, and deadline they want.
- **Asks price:** repeat the fixed ₹499 scope and exclusions; do not invent extra deliverables.
- **Requests custom unpaid work:** offer the prepared sample only; confirm paid scope before creating a custom campaign.
- **No / opt-out:** thank them and stop.
- **No reply:** wait 5–7 business days; send one polite follow-up maximum, then close.
- **Payment:** only record as verified after owner confirmation and actual amount received; never infer payment from an “interested” reply.
