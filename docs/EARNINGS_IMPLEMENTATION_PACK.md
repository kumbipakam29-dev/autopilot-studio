### Day 1 — verify roles

Record date/time checked, exact role title, country eligibility, languages, device, contract type, rate basis, payment rail, minimum payout, fees, assessment, privacy policy and whether the application button works. If any field is unclear, mark UNKNOWN, not PASS.

### Day 2 — prepare truthful profile
Prepare a one-page CV and short profile. Include only actual languages, skills, devices, availability and experience. Do not claim annotation experience or credentials unless true. Never pay for onboarding or a task unlock.

### Day 3 — complete one suitable application
Choose one role that passes the eligibility and payout checklist. User must submit the application and any identity verification themselves. Mark APPLIED only after submission confirmation is visible; save confirmation date/reference without storing sensitive ID documents in the repository.

### Day 4 — create a content-service sample
Use a fictional business and clearly label every asset SPEC WORK — NOT A CLIENT. Create 3 social post concepts, 2 Reels scripts (14–20 seconds), captions and a 7-day calendar. Do not invent results or testimonials.

### Day 5 — shortlist 5 real prospects
Choose businesses with a public business contact route and a visible, relevant content need. Record source URL, observed public signal, personalization reason and date checked. Do not scrape private data or mass-message.

### Day 6 — owner-reviewed outreach
Prepare a tailored message for each prospect. No messages are sent until the owner approves the exact recipients and copy. Respect opt-outs and platform rules.

### Day 7 — review evidence
Count applications actually submitted, assessments received, replies, approved tasks, and money actually received. Keep separate columns for expected, pending and paid. Do not treat advertised rates, applications or client interest as earnings.

## Application readiness checklist
A role is APPLICATION-READY only when:
- Official domain and exact listing are confirmed.
- India eligibility and required language/skills are explicit.
- Work type, rate basis and task acceptance criteria are understood.
- Payout method is available to the applicant in India; minimums, fees and currency conversion are understood.
- No registration, training, deposit or task-unlock payment is required.
- Privacy policy and contracting entity are checked.
- Applicant's answers are truthful and owner-approved.

## Evidence states
DISCOVERED → ELIGIBILITY-CHECKED → APPLICATION-READY → APPLIED → ASSESSMENT → ACCEPTED → WORK-APPROVED → PAYMENT-VERIFIED.
Keep separate terminal states NOT-ELIGIBLE, CLOSED, NO-REPLY and BLOCKED. Never jump stages. A screenshot/confirmation from the actual platform is needed for APPLIED or later states; a bank/payment receipt reviewed by the owner is required for PAYMENT-VERIFIED.
