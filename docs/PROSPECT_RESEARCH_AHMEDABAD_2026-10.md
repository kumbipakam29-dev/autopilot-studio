## Qualification workflow (manual review required)

1. Open the official source and confirm the business still operates under that name.
2. Review its current public Instagram/Facebook presence and note one **specific, accurate** content opportunity. Do not assume the account is inactive or its marketing is poor without checking.
3. Draft one tailored sample post, marked as a concept rather than client work.
4. Generate a respectful one-to-one outreach draft; do not send until a human reviews the facts and approves the channel/message.
5. Track outcomes separately: researched → qualified → draft ready → user approved → sent by user → reply received → paid only after actual payment is confirmed.

## Guardrails

- This file contains public-source leads only; none is marked contacted, interested, qualified, or converted.
- No outreach has been sent and no posts have been published.
- Do not bulk-message these businesses. Check platform rules, use a relevant business contact channel, and respect opt-outs.
- Do not claim results, case studies, or prior client work. Portfolio samples must be labelled fictional spec work.
- Recheck source pages before use; details and operating status can change.
