## Contact and follow-up rules
1. Personalize the opening sentence from a fact on the business's current public site/profile.
2. Prefer a published business email/contact form or business enquiry channel; do not scrape private personal details.
3. Send one relevant introduction only. If there is no response, at most one polite follow-up after 5–7 business days, then stop.
4. Respect “no” or opt-out immediately. No bulk unsolicited messages, repeated WhatsApp blasts, fake urgency or misleading claims.
5. Do not mark a lead “contacted” until a human actually sends the message; record response only when received.
6. Keep private lead notes in the private tracker, not a public repository.
7. Before accepting payment, confirm deliverables, timing, revision scope, and the payment method with the client. Never store payment credentials in this repo.

## Measurement
Track: researched → manually reviewed → contacted → replied → interested → scope agreed → paid. Keep each stage evidence-based. Current status for every business above: **researched only**.
