# Short-Form Video Quality Gate — AutoPilot Digital Studio

**Purpose:** Stop publishing or showing weak sample videos to prospects. This is a production checklist, not a claim that any video has been rendered or tested.

## Non-negotiable canvas
- Export vertical **9:16, 1080 × 1920**, MP4, H.264 where supported.
- Keep all important text, logos, faces and product details inside a central safe area. For cross-platform drafts, use a conservative inset: at least 180 px from top, 380 px from bottom, 60 px from left, and 150 px from right. Treat this as a cautious working guide, not a guarantee; each platform UI changes.
- Never place subtitles against the bottom edge. Keep them above the caption/username region and away from the right-side action buttons.
- Maximum 2 short lines per text card; break long phrases manually. Use large, high-contrast type and a solid or subtle translucent backing when footage is busy.
- No text, logo, face, or CTA may touch the safe-area guide. Preview at phone size before delivery.

## Story structure for a 14–20 second Reel
1. **0–2 s — Hook:** one concrete, readable line; no generic “Welcome to our page.”
2. **2–6 s — Show:** actual product/service or relevant footage. Avoid unrelated stock clips.
3. **6–12 s — Value:** one useful detail, process step, or customer question. Never invent prices, ingredients, offers, testimonials, or results.
4. **12–17 s — Proof/detail:** close-up, process, menu/product item only if verified, or a simple visual demonstration.
5. **17–20 s — CTA:** one modest next step such as “Save this for later” or “Message to ask about availability.” Do not imply the business approved the spec sample.

## Visual quality gate
- Prefer real client-provided or properly licensed footage. If none is available, label as a concept/storyboard and do not present unrelated stock as the business's real premises or products.
- Every shot must support the line being said. Remove filler transitions, random zooms, excessive effects, fake UI, and decorative animations that reduce readability.
- Use no more than one transition style per video; simple cuts are the default.
- Audio must be clear and licensed/royalty-free. Add captions, check spelling, and avoid music drowning out speech.
- Check the opening 2 seconds, every text card, and the final CTA frame at phone size.

## Final QA checklist
- [ ] No clipped, overflowing, or off-canvas text.
- [ ] Text fits within the safe area and has comfortable padding.
- [ ] Spelling, punctuation, business name, and factual details checked.
- [ ] No invented price, offer, review, customer, menu item, or performance claim.
- [ ] Footage is relevant and licensed or supplied by the client.
- [ ] Captions are readable and not covered by platform UI.
- [ ] Video feels coherent with the audio muted and with sound on.
- [ ] Export plays from start to finish; aspect ratio and resolution checked.
- [ ] A human reviewed the actual rendered file before it is sent to a prospect.
- [ ] Spec work is clearly marked **UNCOMMISSIONED CONCEPT — NOT APPROVED OR PUBLISHED BY THE BUSINESS**.

## Fail rule
If any checkbox fails, mark the asset **NEEDS REVISION** and do not show it as a finished portfolio sample. Do not claim a video is fixed until the exported file has actually been reviewed.
