# Ahmedabad First-Campaign Pack — AutoPilot Digital Studio

**Purpose:** turn the studio's social-content offer into a small, repeatable organic outreach test.
**Status:** draft campaign materials only. No message has been sent and no result is claimed.
**Budget:** designed for ₹0 software spend. Mobile data, time, hosting, and platform access can still have costs/limits.

## Offer to test (not a guaranteed result)
**7-Day Content Starter — proposed ₹499**
- 7 post ideas with platform-aware captions
- 2 short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)
- One simple weekly calendar
- One revision round after client feedback
- Client supplies or approves all business facts, offers, images, and final publishing

For the first one or two suitable prospects, offer one sample post concept at no charge before proposing the paid package. Do not promise reach, followers, leads, or sales.

## Priority niches for the first test
Start with businesses where visual content is naturally useful and the owner can approve copy quickly:
1. Cafés, bakeries, small restaurants
2. Home cleaning, repair, and other local services
3. Salons and independent beauty businesses
4. Tutors and coaching centres
5. Fitness studios and yoga instructors
6. Pet grooming and pet services

These are hypotheses to test, not verified demand rankings. Track response rate before expanding to the next city or niche.

## Personalized outreach drafts

Hello [Business Name] team — I liked the way you present [specific menu item / public detail]. I had an idea for a short Reel showing [specific, truthful idea] and a matching caption that could help customers understand [menu / ordering / location]. I can send one sample concept for you to review, with no obligation. Would that be useful? If not, no worries.

### Local service
Hello [Business Name] team — I noticed your public page mentions [specific service]. A simple weekly content series could answer common customer questions, show your real process, and make it easier for people to understand how to enquire. I can share one sample post idea tailored to [service]. Would you like me to send it? No worries if not.

### Salon / fitness / tutor
Hello [Business Name] team — your public page highlights [specific, verified service/class]. I’m preparing content ideas for local businesses and thought of a short [FAQ / process / beginner tip] post that may be useful to your audience. May I send one sample idea? If not, I won’t follow up.

**Use:** replace every bracket with a real observation from the business's public profile. Send individually through a channel the business uses for enquiries. Respect platform policies and any opt-out. Do not bulk-send or scrape contact data.

## Sample concepts (fictional spec work — do not represent as clients)

### Café: Ember & Bean Café
1. **“Three details in our signature coffee”** — close-up shots of grind, pour, and finished cup. Verify the drink and process with the real café before using.
2. **“A quiet corner for your next catch-up”** — show a real seating area and hours; avoid claiming availability unless confirmed.
3. **“Menu spotlight”** — one menu item, ingredients confirmed by the café, price only if current.

### Home cleaning: BrightNest Home Cleaning
1. **“Before a booking: what to prepare”** — three practical preparation tips, no fear-based claims.
2. **“What a standard visit includes”** — show only services actually offered; clarify exclusions.
3. **“Frequently asked question”** — answer one verified question about coverage, booking, or timing.

## First-week execution plan
- **Day 1:** review the website/offer and choose one niche. Check that the contact method and prices are accurate.
- **Day 2:** prepare two clearly labelled spec samples and one short portfolio page.
- **Day 3:** manually review 10 public business profiles in Ahmedabad; record only public business details in a private tracker.
- **Day 4:** write 5 individualized outreach drafts. Quality over volume.
- **Day 5:** send only messages personally approved by the business owner/operator of this studio; do not automate sending.
- **Day 6:** answer replies; provide a tailored sample only when relevant and within scope.
- **Day 7:** record actual replies, opt-outs, requests, paid orders, and revenue. If no replies, revise niche/message before increasing volume.

This is a proposed schedule, not a report that these actions have happened.

## Private tracker schema
Create and keep this in a private spreadsheet or local file, not the public repository:
`business_name, niche, city, public_profile_url, specific_observation, contact_channel, draft_status, sent_date, reply_status, opt_out, next_action, quoted_price, payment_verified, notes`

Do not commit personal contact details, phone numbers, emails, private conversations, customer information, or a real prospect list to this public repository.

## Qualification checklist before quoting
- Does the business publish at least occasionally, or have a clear content gap?
- Can the service be described using verified facts?
- Is there a public, appropriate contact channel?
- Can the requested work be delivered within the quoted scope?
- Is the business willing to provide/approve media and factual claims?
- Are revisions and deadline clear?
- Is price/offer written in plain language and accepted before work begins?

## Payment and delivery rules
- No paid work begins until the client accepts scope and price.
- Verify payment in the actual payment account; a screenshot or promise is not proof of cleared funds.
- Never store payment credentials or request OTPs.
- Deliver the agreed files and record only verified outcomes.
