# First-client sales kit — AutoPilot Digital Studio

**Status:** Internal draft. Not sent to any prospect. No client or payment is claimed.
**Budget:** ₹0 upfront.

## Pilot offer to validate
**7-Day Content Starter — proposed ₹499**

Includes:
- 7 post ideas arranged in a 7-day calendar
- 7 editable captions with calls to action
- 2 short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds each)
- One minor revision round

Not included unless separately agreed: filming, graphic/video production, account access, posting, paid ads, customer support, or guaranteed engagement, followers, leads, or sales.

The price is a test offer, not a proven market rate. Confirm scope, deadline, delivery format, and payment terms with the customer before starting.

## 15-minute discovery conversation
1. What is your main product or service?
2. Who is the customer you want to reach?
3. Which platform currently matters most?
4. What should the next seven days of content help explain or promote?
5. What photos/videos and brand details are approved for use?
6. What language and tone fit your customers?
7. What does a useful result look like to you? (Record their goal; never promise it.)

## Objection responses
**“Can you guarantee followers or sales?”**
No. I can prepare clear, consistent content, but audience response depends on many factors. I don't guarantee reach, followers, leads, or sales.

**“Can I see your client results?”**
This is a new pilot service. The current examples are fictional spec samples, clearly labelled as such; they are not client campaigns or results.

**“Can you post it for me?”**
The pilot is for content planning and copy. Publishing/account access would need separate agreement and explicit permissions.

**“Can you make a sample first?”**
Offer one short idea or hook, not the entire paid deliverable. Clearly label it as a sample.

## Qualification rule
Proceed only when:
- The business and contact channel are verified.
- The request is within scope and legal.
- The customer understands the pilot deliverables and limitations.
- Price, deadline, revisions, and payment terms are agreed.
- Any outreach or public post has been approved by the business owner.

## Status tracking definitions
- **Drafted:** prepared but not sent.
- **Approved:** owner approved sending/publishing.
- **Sent/published:** actual action completed and link or evidence recorded.
- **Replied:** real response received.
- **Won:** scope accepted by customer.
- **Delivered:** agreed files sent to customer.
- **Paid:** payment actually received and verified by owner.

Never promote a record to a later state based only on intention or a draft.
