# Private Client Acquisition Tracker Template

Use this blank template to track outreach for the AutoPilot Digital Studio social-content offer. Keep the working copy **private on your own device or in a private workspace**. Do not commit prospect names, personal contact details, message history, or client/payment records to this public repository.

## Suggested workflow
1. Record only a business's public-facing profile and a specific, verifiable content opportunity.
2. Note the source URL and the date you checked it.
3. Draft a short, personalized message; do not send it until the owner approves the final wording and recipient.
4. Respect no-reply and opt-out signals. One polite follow-up at most, then stop.
5. Update the status only when the event actually happens. Never mark a message sent, client won, or payment received without evidence.
6. Treat ₹499 as a test quote, not a guarantee of demand or income. Payment status must be owner-confirmed and must not include bank credentials, OTPs, or payment secrets.

## Status vocabulary
- `research_needed`
- `draft_ready`
- `awaiting_owner_approval`
- `approved_to_contact`
- `contacted`
- `replied`
- `follow_up_due`
- `interested`
- `brief_pending`
- `quoted`
- `work_started`
- `delivered`
- `declined`
- `no_response_closed`
- `opted_out`
- `payment_owner_confirmed`

`approved_to_contact` is not permission to send automatically; the user must still take or explicitly authorize the actual external action. `payment_owner_confirmed` means the owner reported receipt; it is not independent bank/provider verification.

## CSV columns
The CSV header is provided in `CLIENT_ACQUISITION_TRACKER_TEMPLATE.csv`. Keep notes factual, brief, and non-sensitive. Avoid collecting personal data about individual employees or owners unless strictly necessary for an approved business conversation.
