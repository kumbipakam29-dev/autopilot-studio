# Ahmedabad Bulk Prospect Coverage Plan — v1

**Status:** research framework + initial public-directory batch; not a verified complete city list.  
**Budget:** ₹0. No paid lead databases, no purchased lists, no outreach sent by this document.  
**Goal:** build a broad, auditable prospect pool quickly, then spend time only on prospects with a plausible content-service fit.

## Method: broad coverage, shallow first pass

1. Search public listings by **category × neighbourhood**, not one business at a time.
2. Record each candidate with its source and area; mark all directory-only records **DISCOVERED — UNQUALIFIED**.
3. Quickly inspect public business pages/social profiles for signs of a genuine service fit. Do not assume a business needs help just because it appears in a directory.
4. Deep-check only the strongest candidates: current official page, recent content/activity, contact channel published by the business, and a specific content opportunity.
5. Draft individual outreach only after verification. No bulk spam, automated messaging, or sending without user approval.
6. Track replies, meetings, proposals, and payment separately. A listing is not a lead; a lead is not a client; a client is not a verified payment.

## Fast qualification gates

**Hold immediately** if the business cannot be identified reliably, the listing looks closed/duplicate, no public business-facing channel can be verified, or a proposed post would require unsupported claims. For health/finance/legal niches, avoid guarantees and sensitive targeting.

For candidates that pass, score 0–2 each:
- Local fit / serviceability
- Plausible need (specific and evidenced, not guessed)
- Content opportunity (one useful idea grounded in public facts)
- Public business contact route verified
- Recent activity / signs business is operating
- Potential for a repeatable monthly service

Do not treat a high score as permission to contact. It only prioritizes manual review.

## Next batch priorities

- Add coaching/tuition, home services, interiors, auto services, real estate, boutiques, event businesses, and clinics before expanding cafés/salons further.
- For each category, collect several neighbourhoods per pass and deduplicate by business name + official domain/address.
- Where directories have weak or suspicious data, keep the record as a search hint only; do not count it as verified.
- Do not buy databases. Public directories include duplicates, stale listings and unverified entries; advertised record counts are not unique qualified prospects.

## Progress definitions

- **DISCOVERED — UNQUALIFIED:** found in public listing; not yet reviewed.
- **OFFICIAL SOURCE VERIFIED:** official site/profile/contact source confirmed.
- **CONTENT AUDIT COMPLETE:** specific, factual content opportunity documented.
- **READY FOR APPROVAL:** one-to-one draft prepared for user review.
- **CONTACTED:** only after an approved message is actually sent.
- **REPLIED / CALL / PROPOSAL:** record only when evidenced.
- **CLIENT / PAYMENT VERIFIED:** record only after agreement/payment evidence; payment is not verified until the user confirms funds credited.
