**Status:** Offer sheet draft. No client, testimonial or result is implied.

## What you receive

- **3 post concepts:** angle, suggested visual direction, caption and call to action.
- **1 short Reel script:** designed for 14–20 seconds, with scene timings and on-screen text.
- **7-day content calendar:** suggested format, topic and CTA for each day.
- **One revision round:** one consolidated set of reasonable edits to the agreed deliverables.
- **Delivery target:** within 2 business days after the client provides the brief and confirms all business facts.

## What I need from you

1. Business name, service area and preferred language.
2. Main product/service and intended audience.
3. Confirmed prices, offers, opening hours and preferred contact CTA.
4. Any brand assets you own or have permission to use.
5. Tone preferences and claims/topics to avoid.

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, customer lists or private account access. You keep control of publishing.

## What is not included

Posting to your accounts, paid advertising, photography, video editing, replying to customers, follower or sales guarantees, additional revisions, or unverified market research. If you need those, we can discuss scope separately before any commitment.

## How we start

1. You share the brief and confirm the scope.
2. We agree the delivery date and payment timing in writing before work begins.
3. I prepare the drafts and flag any facts that need your confirmation.
4. You review the package and request one consolidated revision.
5. You decide what to publish and when.

The ₹499 amount is an introductory experiment, not a claim about the market's average price. No work begins until both sides agree on scope and payment terms.
