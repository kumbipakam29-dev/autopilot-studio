# Small-business social content portfolio — SPEC WORK

**Important:** Both examples below are fictional businesses created to demonstrate the service. They are not clients, testimonials, published posts, or performance results. Do not present them as real customer work.

## Sample 1 — Café / restaurant

**Fictional brand:** Monsoon Mug Café  
**Audience:** Nearby students, remote workers, couples and weekend visitors  
**Content goal:** Make the menu and atmosphere easier to discover; encourage genuine visits and enquiries  
**Voice:** Warm, local, relaxed; no unsupported claims about ingredients, prices, awards or customer reviews

### Three post concepts and captions

**Post 1 — “Your slow-morning corner”**
- Visual direction: Coffee cup beside a book and window light; use a real café photo supplied by the client.
- On-image line: “Take your morning one sip at a time.”
- Caption: “A little pause can change the pace of your day. ☕ Bring your book, meet a friend, or simply take five quiet minutes for yourself. What’s your ideal café order?”
- CTA: “Tell us your go-to drink.”
- Hashtags: #LocalCafe #CafeMoments #CoffeeBreak

**Post 2 — “Meet the menu”**
- Visual direction: A three-frame carousel: best-selling drink (only if client confirms), a snack, and a clear menu/ordering shot.
- On-image line: “What are you in the mood for?”
- Caption: “Coffee, something chilled, or a snack to go with your catch-up? Browse the menu and find your next café pick. Message us if you’d like help checking today’s options.”
- CTA: “Save this for your next visit.”
- Hashtags: #CafeMenu #FoodAndCoffee #SupportLocal

**Post 3 — “This or that?”**
- Visual direction: Split-screen of two real menu items; label both accurately.
- On-image line: “Team Iced ☀️ or Team Hot ☕?”
- Caption: “We can’t choose for you—but we’d love to know your pick. Comment ‘iced’ or ‘hot’, then tag the friend who owes you a café catch-up.”
- CTA: “Vote in the comments.”
- Hashtags: #CafePoll #CoffeePeople #LocalEats

### Two short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)

**Reel 1 — “A tiny café reset” (18 seconds)**
- 0–3s: Close-up of a cup being placed on the table. Text: “Need a tiny reset?”
- 3–7s: Show the drink being prepared; use real footage only.
- 7–12s: Wide shot of a quiet seat or table; do not imply availability if unknown.
- 12–16s: Close-up of the finished drink. Text: “Make room for a pause.”
- 16–18s: End card: “Save this for your next café break.”
- Caption: “A little pause, one sip at a time. What would you order?”
- Production note: Film only with owner permission; avoid showing customers without consent.

**Reel 2 — “Pick your pairing” (15 seconds)**
- 0–3s: Two menu items side by side. Text: “Which pairing wins?”
- 3–7s: Quick close-up of option A with its confirmed menu name.
- 7–11s: Quick close-up of option B with its confirmed menu name.
- 11–15s: Both items in frame. Text: “A or B? Vote below.”
- Caption: “Your snack-and-sip pairing says a lot. Choose A or B—and tell us why.”
- Production note: Replace placeholders with the café’s actual items and prices only after the owner confirms them.

### Seven-day content calendar

| Day | Format | Topic | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Photo | Slow-morning café mood | Comment your go-to drink |
| 2 | Story poll | Iced vs hot | Vote |
| 3 | Carousel | Menu highlights, owner-verified | Save the menu |
| 4 | Reel | A tiny café reset | Save for later |
| 5 | Photo | Behind-the-scenes drink preparation | Ask about today’s options |
| 6 | Story | This-or-that food pairing | Vote A/B |
| 7 | Reel | Pick your pairing | Tag a café buddy |

**Before publishing:** Owner confirms menu names, current prices, opening hours, permissions for photos/video, and any availability claims.

## Sample 2 — Local service business

**Fictional brand:** ClearNest Home Cleaning  
**Audience:** Busy households, renters, and people preparing for a move  
**Content goal:** Explain service scope, build trust through clear process information, and encourage quote enquiries  
**Voice:** Practical, respectful, reassuring; no unverified “best”, “guaranteed”, medical, or customer-result claims

### Three post concepts and captions

**Post 1 — “A clearer cleaning checklist”**
- Visual direction: Simple checklist graphic: kitchen surfaces, bathroom touchpoints, floors, and agreed priority areas.
- On-image line: “Know what’s on the checklist.”
- Caption: “A good service starts with clear expectations. Before a booking, tell us which areas matter most and confirm the tasks included in your chosen package. Every home and service scope is different.”
- CTA: “Message us to ask about the checklist.”
- Hashtags: #HomeCleaning #HomeCare #LocalServices

**Post 2 — “How booking works”**
- Visual direction: Three steps: Share your needs → Confirm scope and quote → Choose an available slot.
- On-image line: “Three steps. Clear expectations.”
- Caption: “Planning a clean? Share the areas you want help with, confirm the work and price in writing, then choose from the available appointment slots. No guesswork—just a clear plan before the visit.”
- CTA: “Send an enquiry for a quote.”
- Hashtags: #CleaningService #BookingTips #LocalBusiness

**Post 3 — “Small reset, less stress”**
- Visual direction: A tidy desk or kitchen counter photographed with owner permission; avoid fake before/after claims.
- On-image line: “Start with one space.”
- Caption: “Sometimes the easiest start is choosing one priority area. Tell us which room or task is most important, and we’ll confirm whether it fits the service you need.”
- CTA: “What area would you start with?”
- Hashtags: #HomeReset #CleaningHelp #EverydayHome

### Two short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)

**Reel 1 — “What happens after you enquire?” (17 seconds)**
- 0–3s: Text over a clean checklist: “Thinking about booking a clean?”
- 3–7s: Show a sample enquiry form with fictional data only.
- 7–12s: Show three steps: needs → scope/quote → appointment options.
- 12–17s: Text: “Confirm the details before booking. Send us your questions.”
- Caption: “A clear scope helps everyone start on the same page. Ask us what’s included before confirming.”
- Production note: Use the real process and actual contact channel; never show customer addresses or messages.

**Reel 2 — “Choose your priority area” (15 seconds)**
- 0–3s: Text: “If you had 20 minutes, where would you start?”
- 3–6s: Shot 1: kitchen counter.
- 6–9s: Shot 2: bathroom sink.
- 9–12s: Shot 3: living-room floor.
- 12–15s: Text: “Kitchen, bathroom or living room? Comment below.”
- Caption: “Every home has a different priority. Which space would you choose first?”
- Production note: These are content examples, not a promise that a professional service can complete any area in 20 minutes.

### Seven-day content calendar

| Day | Format | Topic | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Carousel | How booking works | Send an enquiry |
| 2 | Story poll | Which room needs attention first? | Vote |
| 3 | Checklist graphic | Confirmed service checklist | Ask what is included |
| 4 | Reel | What happens after you enquire? | Send questions |
| 5 | Photo | Tools/process behind the scenes | Ask about the process |
| 6 | Reel | Choose your priority area | Comment |
| 7 | FAQ post | Scope, quote and appointment questions | Request a quote |

**Before publishing:** Owner confirms the service area, actual tasks included, booking process, availability, pricing, insurance/qualification statements (if any), and permission for all visuals.

## Deliverables this sample demonstrates

- 3 post concepts and captions per fictional business.
- 2 short Reel scripts per business, each designed for 14–20 seconds.
- A 7-day starter calendar per business.
- Clear client fact-check and permission gates.

It does not demonstrate real client results, audience growth, published content, sales, testimonials, or completed paid work.
