# Soul Sisters — Social Content Spec Sample Pack

**SPEC WORK ONLY — NOT COMMISSIONED, APPROVED, OR PUBLISHED.** This is a creative demonstration based on the publicly described café concept. Confirm all menu items, hours, people, prices, offers, and claims with the business before use. No photos or video assets are included; these are shoot-ready plans, not finished videos.

## Creative direction
Warm, human, sisterhood-led storytelling. Natural light, real staff/food footage, clean type, short on-screen copy. Keep every text element inside a 10% safe margin; use one short line at a time, large high-contrast type, and preview on a phone before export. Avoid stock footage and fake customer reactions.

## 3 post concepts
1. **The people behind the place** — carousel: exterior/detail shot, team moment (only with consent), a food/detail frame. Overlay: “A little story behind your next café stop.”
2. **Made with care** — close-up sequence of a real preparation moment. Overlay: “Small details. A lot of care.”
3. **Your next catch-up spot** — table setting, drinks/food actually available, inviting ambience. Overlay: “Save this for your next catch-up.” Do not claim menu items without confirmation.

## Reel 1 — “A little café pause” (16–18 sec)
- 0–3s: exterior/signage or entrance. Text: “Need a little pause?”
- 3–7s: real preparation close-ups, 2–3 cuts.
- 7–12s: table ambience and verified dishes/drinks.
- 12–16s: wide ambience shot. Text: “Make room for a good moment.”
- 16–18s: end card “Soul Sisters • Save for later”
Caption: “Some plans don’t need to be complicated—just a place to slow down and catch up. Save this idea for your next café visit. Check current menu and timings directly with the café.”
Audio: licensed in-app audio or original sound. Shoot vertical 9:16.

## Reel 2 — “Three details to notice” (14–17 sec)
- 0–2s: hook: “Look a little closer.”
- 2–6s: detail shot 1, label “The space”
- 6–10s: detail shot 2, label “The little touches”
- 10–14s: detail shot 3, label “Your table”
- 14–17s: CTA: “Who would you bring?”
Caption: “A few little details can set the mood for a catch-up. Tag the person you’d invite—only use real footage and confirm any displayed products are available.”

3. “Look a little closer—the small details deserve a moment.”
4. “Your next café plan: pick a person, pick a day, and make time.”
5. “Behind every frame is a real moment. More little glimpses soon.”

7. “Send this to the friend you owe a catch-up.”

## Production & quality gate
- Capture original vertical footage; get consent for identifiable people.
- Safe zone: keep text within central 80% of frame, with at least 10% padding on all sides.
- Maximum 5–7 words per overlay; 1–2 lines; avoid placing text over faces or food.
- Use readable contrast, no flashing transitions, and subtitles for speech.
- Export 1080×1920, 9:16; preview on a phone; check spelling, crop, audio rights, and facts.
- These are concepts/scripts only. No finished video, publication, engagement, or client approval is claimed.
