# Soul Sisters — Social Content Spec Sample Pack

> **SPEC WORK ONLY.** This is an unsolicited concept exercise, not commissioned, approved, or published by Soul Sisters. Confirm all menu items, hours, offers, location details, permissions, and brand preferences with the business before use. No sales or performance claims are made.

## Creative direction
Warm, human, sisterhood-led storytelling. Use real footage only with permission; otherwise use neutral café details, hands preparing food, table textures, and text overlays. Avoid invented menu names, prices, customer testimonials, or claims.

## Three post concepts
1. **“A little corner to slow down” — carousel**
   - Frames: exterior/signage (if verified), welcoming corner, table detail, prompt: “Who would you bring here?”
   - Caption: “Some plans don’t need to be big—just good company and a little time to catch up. Tag the person you’d bring along. [Add verified location/booking details before posting.]”
2. **“The people behind the plates” — founder/story post**
   - Use only if the founders are comfortable being featured and approve the story.
   - Caption: “Every small business has a story behind the scenes. We’d love to share ours—one moment at a time. What would you like to know about the people behind Soul Sisters?”
3. **“Your next catch-up plan” — static or carousel**
   - Visual: table setting and friends chatting; do not stage or imply real customers without consent.
   - Caption: “Send this to your catch-up partner and make a little time for each other this week. Save this idea for your next meet-up.”

## Two short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds each)
### Reel 1 — “Pause the scroll, plan the catch-up” (16–18 sec)
- 0–3s: Close-up of a table being set. On-screen: “When did you last catch up properly?”
- 3–7s: Slow pan across approved café details.
- 7–12s: Two cups/plates placed on table; no identifiable guests without consent.
- 12–17s: Wide welcoming shot. On-screen: “Send this to your catch-up person.”
- Audio: licensed platform audio or original ambient sound. CTA: “Save this for later.”
### Reel 2 — “A moment behind the scenes” (15–20 sec)
- 0–3s: Hands arranging a clean workspace. Text: “A little glimpse behind the scenes…”
- 3–8s: Three quick detail shots of verified preparation/service moments.
- 8–14s: Finished table/detail shot, no unverified dish names.
- 14–18s: Brand sign or neutral end card. Text: “What would you like to see next?”
- Only film real operations with permission; do not fabricate founder quotes or customer reactions.

## Seven captions
1. “Good company makes an ordinary day feel special. Who’s your favourite person to catch up with?”
2. “A small behind-the-scenes moment from our day. What would you like us to show next?”
3. “Your reminder to make time for the people you keep saying you’ll meet soon. Send them this.”
4. “Every detail has a little story. Which kind of behind-the-scenes content do you enjoy?”
5. “Help us choose the next post: space details, team story, or preparation moments?”
6. “Save this for the next time you need a simple plan to meet someone.”
7. “Thanks for being part of our community. Tell us what you’d like to see here next.”

## Production and quality rules
- Shoot vertical 9:16; keep all text inside a central safe area (roughly 10% margin from each edge).
- Use 1–2 short lines per screen; large high-contrast text; preview on a phone before export.
- Keep Reel pacing purposeful: 4–6 shots, readable overlays, clear final CTA. Avoid random stock clips, excessive transitions, unrelated music, or generic AI-looking footage.
- Use only original/permissioned visuals and licensed audio. Confirm business name/logo use with the owner.
- Before posting: verify factual details, spelling, legibility, crop, audio rights, and approval.
- Measure saves, shares, profile visits, and qualified enquiries after publishing; do not promise reach or sales.
