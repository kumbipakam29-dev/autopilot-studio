# The Messy Door Cafe — Social Content Spec Sample Pack

**SPEC WORK ONLY — NOT COMMISSIONED, APPROVED, OR PUBLISHED.** A creative sample informed only by the public-facing artistic/interior café positioning. Verify current menu, décor, hours, location details, offers, and availability before using any claim. This file contains concepts and scripts—not rendered videos.

## Creative direction
Playful, artsy, discovery-led. Make the café feel like a small visual detour. Use genuine footage of the actual space and products, quick but calm edits, restrained typography. Keep all text inside a 10% safe margin; never let lines touch the edge or compete with the subject.

## 3 post concepts
1. **A little visual detour** — carousel of 3 genuine interior details. Overlay: “Take the scenic route to your coffee break.”
2. **One visit, three frames** — entrance, table/ambience, verified food close-up. Overlay: “Arrive. Settle in. Enjoy.”
3. **Spot the detail** — close-up photo with a small numbered circle and question. CTA: “What did you notice first?” Do not fabricate a customer quote or review.

## Reel 1 — “A little detour” (15–18 sec)
- 0–2s: quick shot of the entrance. Text: “Your sign to take a detour.”
- 2–6s: two real interior details, steady camera.
- 6–10s: table setting / genuine food footage.
- 10–14s: wider ambience shot.
- 14–18s: end card: “The Messy Door Cafe • Save for your next plan”
Caption: “Sometimes the best plan is a small change of scene. Save this idea for a café break. Check current details with the café before visiting.”

## Reel 2 — “Three frames, one visit” (14–17 sec)
- 0–2s: hook: “Three frames. One little escape.”
- 2–6s: frame 1 — entrance/signage.
- 6–10s: frame 2 — real interior detail.
- 10–14s: frame 3 — verified dish/drink or table scene.
- 14–17s: CTA: “Which frame is your favourite?”
Caption: “A tiny visual tour, made from real moments at the café. Which frame caught your eye? Confirm every item shown is currently available.”

## Production & quality gate
- Film real vertical footage, 9:16, 1080×1920; do not rely on unrelated stock clips.
- Keep every line inside the central 80% of the frame (minimum 10% edge padding).
- One short overlay at a time; aim for 3–6 words and no more than 2 lines.
- Use stable shots, simple cuts, readable contrast, subtitles, and licensed/original audio.
- Review on a phone for overflow, spelling, crop, audio, and factual accuracy before export.
- Concepts only; no actual video rendering, publishing, or client approval is claimed.
