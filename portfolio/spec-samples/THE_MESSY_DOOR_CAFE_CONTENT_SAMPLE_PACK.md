# The Messy Door Cafe — Social Content Spec Sample Pack

> **SPEC WORK ONLY.** Uncommissioned concept exercise; not approved, commissioned, or published by The Messy Door Cafe. Verify current menu, timings, address, facilities, offers, and brand guidelines with the business before any use. No business relationship or results are claimed.

## Creative direction
Playful, artful, atmosphere-first content. Make the space and the visit feel approachable without inventing menu items, prices, customer reviews, or promises. Use real footage only with permission.

## Three post concepts
1. **“Pick your café mood” — carousel**
   - Slides: cozy corner, interior/art detail, table setup, final question.
   - Caption: “Today’s mood: a quiet corner, a little conversation, or a change of scene? Swipe through and tell us which one you’d pick. [Confirm all images show the current café.]”
2. **“Details worth noticing” — photo carousel**
   - Focus on verified interior details, textures, lighting, and décor.
   - Caption: “Sometimes it’s the little details that make a place memorable. Which detail caught your eye first?”
3. **“Make an hour of it” — static/short carousel**
   - Suggest a simple visit plan without promising Wi-Fi, seating availability, or specific services.
   - Caption: “An hour to catch up, pause, or simply change your surroundings. Who would you bring along? Check current opening details before heading over.”

## Two Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)
### Reel 1 — “Three ways to spend an hour” (17–19 sec)
- 0–3s: Doorway or approved exterior shot. Text: “Need a small change of scene?”
- 3–7s: Interior wide shot.
- 7–11s: Close-ups of verified details and a table being set.
- 11–15s: Slow pan across the space; avoid filming guests without consent.
- 15–18s: End card: “Who’s joining you?” CTA: “Send this to your café person.”
- Do not imply seats are available in real time; verify the current location before posting.
### Reel 2 — “The details tour” (14–17 sec)
- 0–2s: Fast opening detail. Text: “Look a little closer.”
- 2–6s: Three crisp detail shots, each about one second.
- 6–11s: Wider shot that gives viewers context.
- 11–15s: Final detail plus question: “Which detail should we show next?”
- Use original/permissioned footage, licensed audio, stable shots, and restrained transitions.

## Seven captions
1. “A change of scene can be a good start. Which corner or detail would you explore first?”
2. “A closer look at the details. What caught your eye?”
3. “Send this to the person you keep planning a café catch-up with.”
4. “Quiet moment or lively catch-up—which kind of café visit do you enjoy?”
5. “We’re planning more little visual tours. What should we show next?”
6. “Save this as a reminder to make time for a small break.”
7. “One place, lots of little details. Tell us which shot was your favourite.”

## Visual quality rules (fixes for text overflow and weak video)
- Shoot/export vertical 9:16 at 1080×1920 when supported.
- Keep text inside a 10% safe margin on all sides; do not place text on the very edge.
- Maximum two short lines per scene; use high contrast and a phone-readable font size. Check the full video on a phone before export.
- Keep each shot steady and relevant; avoid random stock footage, unrelated clips, excessive transitions, stretched images, and generic AI-generated video.
- Use 4–6 purposeful shots, 1–2 seconds each, with an understandable beginning and clear CTA. Keep total length 14–20 seconds.
- Use licensed platform audio or original sound. Never film identifiable customers without consent.
- Before publishing, check spelling, text boundaries, crops, audio, factual details, and owner approval.
- Track saves, shares, profile visits, and enquiries after publishing; no reach or sales guarantees.
