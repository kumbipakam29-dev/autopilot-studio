# Spec Sample Pack — Ahmedabad Café Content

**Portfolio status:** FICTIONAL SPEC WORK — NOT COMMISSIONED BY A REAL BUSINESS.
**Purpose:** Demonstrate what AutoPilot Digital Studio could deliver to a café prospect.
**Not a real client campaign:** Do not imply this was published or produced results.

## Fictional business brief

- Business: “Copper Cup Café” (fictional)
- Audience: local residents, students and people working nearby
- Tone: warm, simple, inviting
- Goal: make the menu and visit experience easier to discover
- Language: English draft; adapt to Gujarati or Hinglish if the actual owner prefers

## Post concept 1 — A little pause in your day

**Visual direction:** Original overhead photo or self-created illustration of a mug beside a notebook. Do not use another café’s image.

**On-image text:** Your little coffee break.

**Caption:**
Some days need a small pause. ☕
Take a breath, choose your favourite cup, and give yourself a few quiet minutes.
What’s your go-to coffee order?

**CTA:** Tell us your favourite in the comments.

**Alt text:** A warm cup of coffee beside an open notebook on a café table.

## Post concept 2 — Menu spotlight

**Visual direction:** Use an original image of a real menu item only after the business confirms the item and supplies/approves the photo.

**On-image text:** Meet your next favourite.

**Caption template:**
Meet the [verified drink name] — made with [verified ingredients or preparation detail].
Prefer it hot or iced? Let us know below.

**Publishing guardrail:** Replace bracketed text only with details confirmed by the business. Never invent ingredients, dietary claims, prices or availability.

## Post concept 3 — Find your café mood

**Visual direction:** Three-panel layout: quiet corner, takeaway cup, friends at a table. Use original/approved imagery.

**On-image text:** Slow morning / Quick pickup / Catch-up time

**Caption:**
What kind of coffee break are you having today?
🌿 A slow morning
🏃 A quick pickup
💬 A catch-up with friends

Pick your mood below.

## Reel script 1 — “Need a reset?” (16 seconds)

- **0–3 sec:** Close-up of coffee being poured. On-screen: “Need a little reset?”
- **3–7 sec:** Show a cup and notebook on a table.
- **7–12 sec:** Detail shot of foam or steam; overlay: “Take a moment for yourself.”
- **12–16 sec:** End frame with fictional brand text “Copper Cup Café” and CTA “What’s your coffee mood?”

**Production note:** Shoot original footage; no customer faces without consent. Use licensed or platform-cleared audio.

## Reel script 2 — “Choose your coffee-break mood” (18 seconds)

- **0–4 sec:** Text: “Your coffee break, your way.”
- **4–8 sec:** Scene 1: quiet cup and book — “Slow it down.”
- **8–12 sec:** Scene 2: takeaway cup — “Keep it moving.”
- **12–15 sec:** Scene 3: two cups on a table — “Catch up.”
- **15–18 sec:** Text: “Which one are you today?”

## Before a real business uses this

1. Confirm the real business name, location, menu, prices and product claims.
2. Replace all fictional names and generic details.
3. Obtain permission for all photos, logos, staff/customer images and testimonials.
4. Have the owner approve final copy before publishing.
5. Keep a record of scope, deadline, revisions and payment terms.
