# WorthTheWheat — Spec Social Content Pack (Draft)

**Portfolio status:** Fictional/spec sample prepared by AutoPilot Digital Studio; not commissioned, endorsed, or approved by WorthTheWheat. Do not present these as published client work.  
**Research note:** The public website describes homemade baked goods and custom cakes. Every specific flavour, price, turnaround time, delivery area, ingredient, offer, and order policy must be confirmed by the business before publication.  
**Usage note:** These are concepts and scripts only. Use only original or owner-approved photos/video and obtain permission before using the business name/logo in a public portfolio.

## Offer being demonstrated
A compact 7-day content starter: content calendar, 7 captions, and 2 short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds). No promise of reach, sales, or follower growth.

## Three post concepts

### 1. Custom cake, from idea to finish
- **Format:** 4–5 slide carousel.
- **Visual sequence:** Customer inspiration (use a clearly labelled placeholder) → approved design detail → decorating process → finished cake → “Ask us about your idea.”
- **Caption:** “A celebration cake often starts with a little idea. 🎂 If you’re planning something special, share the occasion and the style you have in mind. We’ll confirm what’s possible, along with the details and timing, before your order is finalised.”
- **CTA:** “DM to ask about a custom cake.”
- **Pre-publish check:** Confirm enquiry route, lead time, customisation limits and whether customer photos may be shown.

### 2. The making-of moment
- **Format:** Single photo or 3-frame Story.
- **Visual sequence:** Hands preparing/decorating; close-up of a texture/detail; finished bake.
- **Caption:** “A little behind-the-scenes moment from the baking table. ✨ What would you like to see next: decorating, finishing details, or packing an order? Tell us below.”
- **CTA:** “Vote in Stories / comment with your choice.”
- **Pre-publish check:** Film only actual process and ingredients; do not imply a method or ingredient that the baker has not confirmed.

### 3. Custom-order checklist
- **Format:** Saveable carousel.
- **Slides:** Occasion/date; approximate size/servings; preferred design; dietary/allergen questions; delivery or collection question; “Confirm availability before paying.”
- **Caption:** “Planning a custom cake? These details can help start the conversation: occasion, date, approximate size, design references, and any dietary questions. Availability and options need to be confirmed with the bakery before an order is placed.”
- **CTA:** “Save this checklist for later.”
- **Pre-publish check:** Business must approve the actual order process, payment steps and dietary/allergen wording.

## Two short Reel scripts (14–20 seconds)

### Reel 1 — “An idea becomes a cake” (18 seconds)
- **0–3s:** Text overlay: “Have a cake idea in mind?”
- **3–7s:** Close-up of a sketch/reference or approved design detail. Overlay: “Share your inspiration.”
- **7–13s:** Real decorating clips approved by the bakery. Overlay: “Details take shape.”
- **13–17s:** Reveal the finished cake, only if it is an actual approved product.
- **17–18s:** Overlay: “Ask about your custom idea.”
- **Caption:** “Every custom idea starts with a conversation. Send the occasion, date and style you have in mind; we’ll confirm availability and options.”
- **Audio:** Use original audio or music licensed for the intended platform/commercial use.

### Reel 2 — “Before you enquire” (15–17 seconds)
- **0–3s:** Text overlay: “Planning a celebration cake?”
- **3–8s:** Fast text cards: “Date • servings • style”
- **8–12s:** Add: “Dietary questions? Mention them early.”
- **12–16s:** Text overlay: “Ask the bakery to confirm availability.”
- **Caption:** “A few details make it easier to discuss your cake idea. Availability, design and any dietary requirements should always be confirmed directly.”
- **Footage:** Can be made as typography-only video if no approved bakery footage is available.

## Seven-day calendar

| Day | Format | Topic | Goal | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Carousel | Custom cake journey | Explain the idea-to-finish concept | Ask about a custom idea |
| 2 | Story poll | What should we show behind the scenes? | Invite low-friction feedback | Vote |
| 3 | Reel | An idea becomes a cake | Show an approved process | Enquire |
| 4 | Static post | One confirmed product spotlight | Present a real item accurately | Ask about availability |
| 5 | Carousel | Custom-order checklist | Help customers prepare an enquiry | Save this post |
| 6 | Stories | FAQ prompt | Collect common questions | Send a question |
| 7 | Reel or photo | Behind-the-scenes moment | Show real craft/process | Follow or enquire |

## Caption bank for the seven days

1. **Day 1:** “A custom cake starts with your occasion and an idea. Share the date, approximate size and style you have in mind, and ask us to confirm availability and options.”
2. **Day 2:** “Which behind-the-scenes moment would you like to see: decorating, finishing details, or packing? Vote and help choose a future post.”
3. **Day 3:** “From an idea to the finishing details—here’s a look at the process. For a custom order, share your occasion and date so availability can be confirmed.”
4. **Day 4:** “Today’s spotlight: [insert a product name confirmed by the bakery]. Ask the team about current options and availability before placing an order.”
5. **Day 5:** “Saving this for your next celebration? Note the date, servings, design inspiration and any dietary questions before enquiring. Final options need to be confirmed by the bakery.”
6. **Day 6:** “What would you like to know before ordering a custom cake? Leave a question or send it directly. We’ll answer once the details are confirmed.”
7. **Day 7:** “A small behind-the-scenes look at the work that goes into a bake. Would you like more process clips or finished-product close-ups?”

## Simple success tracking (no paid analytics)
Record weekly, from platform-native insights if the owner shares them:
- Posts published (count only after checking the live URL)
- Reach/views and saves/shares, where available
- Profile visits and genuine enquiries, if visible
- Enquiries that mention a post, only when the customer actually says so

Do not promise a sales lift or infer sales from likes/views. Do not access private analytics without the owner's permission.
