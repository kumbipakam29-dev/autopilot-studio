# Lead Research & Personalized Draft Loop

## Purpose
Find relevant small-business prospects, prepare genuinely personalized first-contact drafts, and track every lead without implying contact or outcomes that have not happened.

## Geography and order
1. Ahmedabad
2. Gandhinagar
3. Other Gujarat cities
4. Other Indian cities
5. International prospects

Start with social-media content services: cafés/restaurants, salons/beauty, cleaning/home services, fitness/wellness, local retail, and other small businesses whose public pages show a plausible content need.

## Research checklist per prospect
Record:
- Business name and category
- City/area
- Official website or official public social profile
- Public contact route from an official page (if available)
- Public evidence checked and date checked
- One specific, respectful content opportunity based on visible public information
- Suggested deliverable (post concept, caption, Reel script, or weekly plan)
- Personalized outreach draft
- Status: RESEARCHED — NOT CONTACTED / DRAFT READY / CONTACTED (only after verified sending) / REPLIED / INTERESTED / WON / CLOSED
- Next action and follow-up date, if applicable

Deduplicate by business name + domain/social handle. Do not store private personal data or scrape gated/private sources. Do not guess email addresses, phone numbers, business details, or the owner's needs. If no official contact route is found, mark it as unavailable and do not invent one.

## Personalization rules
- Mention one real, observable detail from a public business page; avoid unsupported criticism.
- Offer one small, relevant idea, not a full unsolicited free campaign.
- Keep the first message concise, permission-based, and easy to decline.
- Never send automatically as part of this workflow.
- Never claim a sample was made for them, a message was sent, or a result was achieved unless true.
- Respect opt-outs and do not repeatedly message non-responders.

## Draft template
Subject: A content idea for [Business Name]

Hi [Business Name] team,

I came across [specific public detail that has been verified]. One content idea that may fit is [one concrete, low-effort idea] to help customers understand [relevant menu item/service/offer].

AutoPilot Digital Studio prepares social captions, short Reel scripts, and weekly content plans for small businesses. If useful, may I share a short sample idea tailored to your public offering?

No pressure either way. Thanks,  
AutoPilot Digital Studio

## Daily batch target (guideline, not a claim of automation)
- Research 5–10 prospects with public evidence.
- Draft a distinct first-contact message for each verified prospect.
- Review for accuracy, tone, duplicates, and contact route.
- Save the batch and leave every unsent message marked NOT CONTACTED.
- Prioritize relevance over volume; never pad counts with unverified leads.

## Weekly review
Track researched, draft-ready, contacted (verified), replies, interested, proposals, paid clients, and verified payments separately. Do not treat research or drafts as outreach, and do not treat interest as revenue.
