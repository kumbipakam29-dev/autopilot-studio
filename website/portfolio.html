SAMPLE 01 · CAFÉ

Juniper Corner Café

Fictional neighbourhood café concept

Post concept 2 — Behind the cup

Visual: 3-frame carousel showing beans, brewing and the finished cup. Caption: “Good coffee is a few thoughtful steps. Here’s a peek at ours.”

SAMPLE 02 · LOCAL SERVICE

BrightNest Home Care

Fictional home-cleaning service concept

Post concept 1 — Before you book

Visual: checklist graphic. Caption: “Before booking a home clean, confirm the rooms included, estimated duration, products used and any add-on charges.”

Post concept 2 — Quick weekly reset

Visual: simple 4-step checklist. Caption: “A small reset can make the week feel easier: clear surfaces, sort laundry, refresh high-touch areas and plan one deeper-clean task.”

Post concept 3 — Service explained

Visual: carousel explaining standard clean vs deep clean. Caption: “Not sure which service fits? Compare what’s included and ask for a clear quote before booking.”

Reel script · 18 seconds

0–4s: Show a tidy checklist. Text: “Booking a cleaning service?” 4–10s: Show room checklist, time estimate and supplies. Voiceover: “Ask what’s included, how long it takes, and whether supplies cost extra.” 10–15s: Show booking notes. Text: “Clear scope = fewer surprises.” 15–18s: “Save this checklist for later.”

7-day calendar

Mon: booking checklist · Tue: FAQ story · Wed: standard vs deep clean carousel · Thu: maintenance tip · Fri: 18-sec Reel · Sat: supplies FAQ · Sun: next-week cleaning planner.
