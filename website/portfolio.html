Ideas that make posting feel easier.

A look at how we structure social content for small businesses: clear post concepts, practical short-video scripts, captions with calls to action, and a simple weekly calendar.

Both businesses below are fictional. These are demonstration samples—not paid client work, testimonials, published campaigns or claims of results.

01 · Cafe / restaurant

Ember & Bean Café

Goal: give nearby customers reasons to discover the menu and save the café for a future visit.

Post 1 — Slow-morning ritual

Visual direction: warm coffee close-up, pastry, natural morning light.

Caption: “A slower morning starts here ☕ Pick your favourite cup, pair it with a little treat, and give yourself five minutes to enjoy the moment.”

CTA: “Tag your coffee-break partner.”

Post 2 — Meet the menu favourite

Visual direction: one signature drink, ingredients and flavour notes.

Caption: “Smooth, comforting, and made for your next little break. Meet the drink we’d put on your must-try list.”

CTA: “Save this for your next visit.”

Post 3 — A little reset

Visual direction: cosy corner, table setup, friends catching up.

Caption: “Emails can wait a moment. Catch up with a friend over something good and make room for a proper break.”

CTA: “Who’s joining you this week?”

Reel A · 16 sec

0–3s: coffee pour, text “Need a little reset?”
3–8s: pastry and cosy corner
8–13s: drink close-up
13–16s: “Save this for your next coffee break.”

Reel B · 18 sec

0–4s: “What’s in today’s cup?”
4–10s: espresso and milk being prepared
10–15s: finished drink reveal
15–18s: “Coffee break, sorted.”

02 · Local service business

BrightNest Home Cleaning

Goal: explain the service clearly, answer common questions, and invite enquiries without overpromising.

Post 1 — Room-by-room reset

Visual direction: a simple checklist graphic for kitchen, living room and bedroom.

Caption: “Not sure where to start? Pick one room, set a small goal, and work through the basics. A little structure can make a busy day feel more manageable.”

CTA: “Save this checklist for later.”

Post 2 — What’s included?

Visual direction: clear list of included tasks, with scope confirmed before booking.

Caption: “Clear expectations make a smoother clean. Ask us about the tasks, timing and supplies included in the service before choosing a slot.”

CTA: “Send your questions before booking.”

Post 3 — Before your appointment

Visual direction: three easy preparation tips.

Caption: “A few small steps can help your cleaning appointment run smoothly: secure valuables, share priority areas, and let us know about any special instructions.”

CTA: “Message to ask about availability.”

Reel A · 15 sec

0–3s: busy counter, text “Short on time?”
3–8s: tidy-up sequence
8–12s: clean final shot
12–15s: “Ask about available service slots.”

Reel B · 20 sec

0–4s: “Three things to prep before a clean”
4–13s: clear surfaces, secure valuables, share priorities
13–17s: checklist recap
17–20s: “Save this for your next booking.”
